# Bah.Digital business plan

> **Sell outcomes, not "AI".** Not "we build chatbots", but "we answer your
> customers 24/7, qualify leads and pass the complex cases to your staff."

All prices are **starting hypotheses** in USD. Adjust them for each client's
location, scope and the value delivered. Local Sierra Leone clients will often
pay less than international clients. Sell into both markets.

## 1. Launch offers (sell these first)

| # | Offer | What the client gets | Price | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **AI Business Audit** | Report: where AI/automation saves time and money, ROI estimates, 90-day plan | $50 to $300 | 2 to 5 days |
| 2 | **AI Customer Assistant** | Website/WhatsApp assistant trained on their business, with lead capture | $200 to $1,500 setup + $50 to $300/mo | 1 to 2 weeks |
| 3 | **Automation Package** | 1 to 3 workflows (leads → CRM → follow-up, invoices, reports) | $250 to $2,000 + $50 to $300/mo | 1 to 3 weeks |
| 4 | **Digital Growth Package** | Website + social content + email + monthly report | $300 to $1,500/mo | ongoing |
| 5 | **AI Transformation** | Audit + selected systems built + staff training | $1,500 to $10,000+ | 1 to 3 months |

**The audit is your door-opener.** It is cheap to buy, and every report points
to offers 2 to 5.

## 6. Getting paid

| Customer | Method |
|---|---|
| Sierra Leone | **Orange Money** (apply for a merchant account), Afrimoney, bank transfer |
| International: services | Wise / Payoneer invoices, PayPal |
| International: digital products | Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy / Payhip (they handle cards + tax) |
| International: SaaS | a merchant-of-record (Lemon Squeezy / Paddle). Confirm Sierra Leone payout support before relying on any gateway |

Check each provider's current country support yourself before building on it,
because this changes. The SaaS code will use a **payment layer** so providers
can be swapped.

**Every deal:** written scope, 50% deposit, milestones, revision limit, and the
balance before final handover.

## 8. First customers

Restaurants, hotels and guesthouses, schools, NGOs, clinics (data protection
needed), real estate, construction, shops, churches, consultants, startups and
creators.

**Green Vital is client #1:** build it there, document it as a case study, then sell it.

## 9. First 30 days of selling

1. Create a one-page offer sheet for the AI Business Audit (Canva).
2. Run a free audit for Green Vital and 2 friendly businesses. Turn them into case studies.
3. Post daily on LinkedIn/Facebook/TikTok about what you are building ("building in public").
4. Message 10 local businesses a day with a specific improvement idea for them.
5. Launch the newsletter with a free lead magnet (e.g. "20 AI automations for Sierra Leone businesses").
