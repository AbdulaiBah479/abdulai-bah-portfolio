# Journal review
Read journal files in `journal/` (not .env). Compute: trades, win rate, avg win/loss in R, expectancy, profit factor, max drawdown, performance by setup/market/time/day, plan-adherence %, cost drag. Identify behavioural patterns (revenge trades, oversizing, early exits). Give 3 specific rules for next week. Flag small samples (<30 trades) as inconclusive.
