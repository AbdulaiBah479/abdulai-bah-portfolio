# 03 · Stocks, shares & ETFs

## Core ideas
- A share = fractional ownership; return = price change + dividends. Long-run equity returns exist because you carry risk; there's no free alpha.
- **Index ETFs** (global/US total market) beat most active managers after fees over 10+ years (SPIVA data). Make this your baseline.
- Costs: expense ratio (TER), bid/ask spread, commissions, FX conversion, stamp duty/taxes, tracking error.

## Instruments
Common stock · ETF (basket, trades like a stock) · Mutual/index fund · REIT · ADR · Preferred · IPO (high risk) · Penny stock (avoid) · Fractional shares.

## Account types (country-specific—check yours)
Taxable brokerage · tax-advantaged (ISA/SIPP UK, Roth/401k/IRA US, TFSA/RRSP CA, super AU, PEA FR…). Use the tax-advantaged wrapper first.

## Choosing a broker
Regulation & investor protection (SIPC, FSCS, ICCF…) · fees · FX fee · markets offered · fractional shares · tax docs · API. Examples (check availability by country): Interactive Brokers, Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard, Trading 212, Freetrade, eToro, Alpaca (API, US), Revolut. Paper trading: Alpaca, IBKR, TradingView, Thinkorswim.

## Analysis frameworks
**Fundamental**: revenue/earnings growth, margins, FCF, ROIC, debt/EBITDA, interest cover, moat, management, valuation (P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/FCF, PEG, DCF). See `cheatsheets/fundamentals-ratios.md`.
**Technical**: trend (50/200 MA), RS vs index, volume, breakouts, support/resistance.
**Quant/factor**: value, momentum, quality, low-vol, size. Factor premiums are real but lumpy; cost-sensitive.

## Strategies
1. Core-satellite: 80% ETFs + 20% stock ideas.
2. DCA monthly into index ETFs (automate).
3. Dividend growth (see 04).
4. Momentum / trend following on ETFs: hold if price > 200-day MA, else bonds/cash (simple, reduces deep drawdowns, may lag).
5. Swing trading (days–weeks) with strict stops — needs a tested edge.
6. Earnings/IPO plays — gambling for most.

## Pre-buy checklist
Do I understand how it makes money? Valuation vs history/peers? Debt manageable? Insider/dilution? Catalyst/risk? Position ≤ 5% of portfolio? Stop/exit thesis written? Is this beating just buying the index?

## Market mechanics
Hours (NYSE 14:30–21:00 UTC; LSE 08:00–16:30 UK) · pre/post-market thin liquidity · settlement T+1 (US) · short selling & margin = unlimited loss/margin calls · pattern-day-trader rule (US, <$25k margin accounts) · corporate actions: splits, dividends, spin-offs, mergers · earnings dates.
