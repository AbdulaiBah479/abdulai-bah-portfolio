# 06 · Other instruments

| Instrument | What | Main danger | Starter stance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Options** | Right to buy (call) / sell (put) at strike by expiry | Time decay, 100% loss of premium; short options = large/unlimited loss | Learn with paper; defined-risk only (spreads, cash-secured puts, covered calls) |
| **Futures** | Leveraged contract on index/commodity/rate | Leverage, margin calls, rollover | Micro contracts (MES, MNQ) after months of paper |
| **Commodities** (gold, oil, ags) | Via ETFs/futures | Contango roll cost, geopolitics | Small gold/diversified exposure via ETF |
| **CFDs / spread betting** | Derivatives on price | High retail loss rate, overnight financing | Avoid |
| **REITs** | Property shares | Rate sensitivity | Via diversified ETF |
| **Crypto ETFs/ETPs** | Spot BTC/ETH exposure inside brokerage | Tracking, fees | Easier tax/custody than coins in some countries |
| **Leveraged/inverse ETFs** | Daily 2–3× | Path-dependent decay | Not for holding |
| **P2P lending, SPACs, NFTs, penny/pump tokens** | | Fraud, illiquidity | Avoid |
| **Savings / MMFs / CDs** | Cash yield | Inflation | Park dry powder here |

## Options cheat essentials
- Greeks: Delta (price sensitivity ≈ prob ITM), Gamma, Theta (daily decay), Vega (volatility), Rho.
- Implied volatility rank: sell premium when IV rank high, buy when low (not a guarantee).
- Covered call = own 100 shares + short call (income, caps upside). Cash-secured put = paid to wait to buy.
- Vertical spread defines max loss. Never sell naked options without deep experience.
- Payoff at expiry: long call = max(S−K,0)−premium; breakeven = K+premium.
