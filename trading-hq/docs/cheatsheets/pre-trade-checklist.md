# Pre-trade checklist (print this)

- [ ] Is this in my written plan/strategy? (If not: stop.)
- [ ] Higher-timeframe trend/regime? With or against it?
- [ ] Key level / catalyst / why now?
- [ ] Spread & liquidity OK? No major news within 30 min?
- [ ] Entry ____ Stop ____ Target ____ → R:R ≥ 1:2?
- [ ] Size by formula; risk ≤ 1% ($____); total open risk ≤ 5%?
- [ ] Correlated with existing positions?
- [ ] Daily/weekly loss limit not hit? Am I calm, rested, not chasing?
- [ ] Stop order placed on the platform?
- [ ] What invalidates the idea? (written)
- [ ] Journal entry opened (screenshot).

# Post-trade
- [ ] Followed plan? (Y/N) — separate "good process" from "good outcome".
- [ ] R result, fees, slippage.
- [ ] Emotion, lesson, screenshot of exit.
