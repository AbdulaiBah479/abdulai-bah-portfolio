# Dividend dates, DRIP & tax notes

```
Declare ──► Ex-dividend ──► Record ──► Pay
            (buy before this day to get paid; price gaps down ≈ dividend)
```
US: ex-date is typically 1 business day before record date (T+1 settlement).

- Qualified vs ordinary dividends (US): qualified need holding period (>60 days in the 121-day window around ex-date).
- Foreign withholding: file the treaty form (e.g. W-8BEN for US stocks as non-US person); ADRs may have fees.
- ETFs: distributing vs accumulating share classes (UCITS Acc/Dist). Accumulating may still be taxable in some countries.
- DRIP: automatically reinvests; each reinvestment is a new tax lot (record keeping).
- Payout months: quarterly (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct, Feb/May/Aug/Nov, Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec cycles); monthly payers (REITs, some ETFs). Build a calendar for smooth income.
- Cuts: look for payout >100%, falling FCF, rising debt, sector stress, management language ("review of capital allocation").
- Tax-advantaged accounts shelter dividends in many countries; foreign withholding may still apply.

Country quick pointers (verify yourself): US qualified-dividend rates; UK dividend allowance + ISA; Canada eligible dividend tax credit; Australia franking credits; many EU countries withhold at source.
