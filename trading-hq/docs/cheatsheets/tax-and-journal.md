# Tax records & journal

Not tax advice; rules vary by country. Typical taxable events: selling for profit, crypto-to-crypto swaps, spending crypto, staking/earn rewards, dividends, interest, FX gains (some jurisdictions), futures/CFD gains.

## Keep
Date/time, instrument, side, qty, price, fees, account/exchange, base-currency value at the time, wallet transfers, dividend/interest statements, cost-basis method (FIFO/specific ID/average, per local law).

## Tools
Exchange CSV exports/Binance tax reports, Koinly / CoinTracking / Awaken / CoinLedger (paid, free tiers), broker 1099/year-end statements, spreadsheet. Open-source: `rotki/rotki` (local-first portfolio & tax tracker).

## Wash-sale / loss harvesting
Rules differ (US wash-sale for stocks; crypto rules evolving; UK 30-day bed-and-breakfast rule). Check local law before harvesting losses.

## Journal fields (see `journal/TEMPLATE.md`)
Date · market · setup · side · entry · stop · target · size · risk $ · exit · R · fees · followed plan · emotion · screenshot · lesson. Weekly review: win rate, avg R, expectancy, rule breaks, best/worst setups, time-of-day.
