## DCF in one paragraph
Value = Σ FCFₜ/(1+r)ᵗ + terminal value. Terminal = FCF×(1+g)/(r−g), g ≤ GDP growth. Highly sensitive: run bear/base/bull and demand a margin of safety (≥ 20–30%).

## Where to find numbers
10-K / 10-Q / annual reports (SEC EDGAR, Companies House) · earnings call transcripts · investor presentations · Yahoo/Stockanalysis for summaries.

## Red flags
Revenue up but cash flow down · rising receivables/inventory vs sales · frequent "one-time" charges · auditor changes · high insider selling · debt maturities wall · aggressive non-GAAP metrics · serial acquirer.

## Macro dashboard
Policy rates · CPI/PCE · unemployment · yield curve (10y−2y) · credit spreads · PMI · USD index · VIX · oil/gold · liquidity (M2, Fed balance sheet).
