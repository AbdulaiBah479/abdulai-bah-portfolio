# Candlesticks & chart patterns

Candle = open, high, low, close. Body = open→close; wicks = rejected prices. Context (trend, level, volume) matters more than the shape. Most patterns have weak standalone edge.

## Single/dual candles
| Pattern | Look | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Doji | open≈close | Indecision |
| Hammer / Hanging man | small body, long lower wick | Hammer at lows = bullish; hanging man at highs = bearish |
| Shooting star / Inverted hammer | long upper wick | Rejection of highs |
| Marubozu | full body, no wicks | Strong momentum |
| Engulfing | body engulfs prior body | Reversal at a level |
| Pin bar | long wick through level, close back | Level rejection |
| Inside bar | range inside prior bar | Compression → breakout |
| Morning/Evening star | 3-candle reversal | Needs confirmation |
| Three white soldiers / black crows | 3 strong candles | Trend continuation/start |

## Chart patterns
- **Continuation**: flags, pennants, ascending/descending triangles, rectangles, cup & handle.
- **Reversal**: head & shoulders (and inverse), double/triple top/bottom, rising/falling wedges.
- Measured move ≈ height of pattern projected from breakout. Volume should expand on breakout.
- Failure rate is high; trade the retest, place the stop beyond the pattern.

## Support/resistance
Prior highs/lows, round numbers, high-volume nodes, moving averages (50/200), Fibonacci 38.2/50/61.8%, previous day/week/month highs/lows, VWAP. Broken resistance often becomes support.

## Trend
Uptrend = higher highs + higher lows. Break of the last higher low = first warning. Trade with higher-timeframe trend.
