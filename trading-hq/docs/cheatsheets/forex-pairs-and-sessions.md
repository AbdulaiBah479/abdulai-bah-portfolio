## High-impact events
FOMC/ECB/BoE/BoJ rate decisions · NFP (first Friday) · CPI · retail sales · GDP · central-bank speeches · intervention warnings. Check the calendar every morning; stay flat or reduce size.

## Correlations
EUR/USD ≈ −USD/CHF; AUD/NZD move together; USD/CAD vs oil inverse; long EUR/USD + long GBP/USD ≈ doubled USD risk.

## Interest-rate carry
Long high-yielder vs low-yielder earns swap (AUD/JPY, USD/JPY classics) but unwinds violently in risk-off.

## Checklist
