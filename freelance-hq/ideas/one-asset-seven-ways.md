# One asset, seven ways (the multiplier)

The smartest beginners do not build seven different things. They build ONE thing well, then package it seven ways. Worked example below, using your portfolio's "AI Chatbot System".

## The asset
"An AI chatbot that answers customer questions from a clinic's documents and captures appointment requests."

## Seven packages from the same work
| # | Package | Who pays | Where | Effort after the first build |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Done-for-you service**: "AI chatbot for your clinic, 5 days" | Clinics | Fiverr, Upwork, direct | Low (reuse build) |
| 2 | **Productized audit**: "Chatbot readiness audit" | Clinics/others | Direct, Fiverr | Low |
| 3 | **Starter kit** (code + prompts + deploy guide + FAQ test set) | Developers, agencies | Gumroad/Payhip, GitHub Sponsors | Medium once |
| 4 | **Template pack**: knowledge-base templates, conversation flows, escalation scripts | Non-technical owners | Etsy/Gumroad | Low |
| 5 | **Ebook**: "Build your first business chatbot (no jargon)" | Beginners | KDP, direct | Medium once |
| 6 | **Mini-course**: screen-recorded build + workbook | Learners | Gumroad/Udemy/Payhip | Medium once |
| 7 | **Content engine**: posts, short videos, newsletter about each step | Audience | LinkedIn, YouTube, email | Low weekly |

## The flow
```
Real client or personal project
        |
        v
Sanitized case study  ---> posts, portfolio, proposals
        |
        v
Reusable kit  ---> product page
        |
        v
Ebook (lead magnet) ---> email list ---> course ---> service upsell
```

## Sanitizing checklist (before turning client work into a product)
- [ ] Client permission, or build from your own demo data
- [ ] No names, logos, keys, private data, internal URLs
- [ ] All third-party licences checked
- [ ] Client's confidential logic generalised
- [ ] README with exact steps, tested on a clean machine
