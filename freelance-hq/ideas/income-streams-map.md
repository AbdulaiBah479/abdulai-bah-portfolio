# Income streams map: all the places money can come from

## Stream types
| Stream | Examples | Effort per $ | Speed | Scalability | Beginner priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marketplace gigs | Fiverr, Upwork, Freelancer, PeoplePerHour, Contra | High at first | Fast | Medium | 1 |
| Direct service clients | Local businesses, agencies, referrals | Medium | Medium | Medium-high | 2 |
| Retainers | Care plans, content ops | Low after setup | Medium | High | 3 |
| Digital products | Templates, kits, presets, code, prompt packs | High build, then low | Slow | High | 3 |
| Ebooks | KDP, Kobo, D2D, direct | High build, then low | Slow | Medium | 4 |
| Courses/workshops | Gumroad, Udemy, live cohorts | High build | Slow | High | 5 |
| Audience monetisation | Sponsors, affiliates, memberships | Needs audience | Slowest | High | 6 |
| Open-source / bounties | Sponsorship, paid issues | Skill-dependent | Variable | Medium | Optional |
| Micro-SaaS | Small paid tools | Highest | Slowest | Highest | Later |

## Platforms by stream
- **Services:** Fiverr, Upwork, Freelancer.com, PeoplePerHour, Contra, Toptal/Braintrust (vetted), Wellfound, LinkedIn, local networking, Malt (EU), Guru.
- **Products:** Gumroad, Payhip, Ko-fi, Lemon Squeezy, Sellfy, Whop, Etsy, Creative Market, Envato, Notion/Canva/Figma/Framer/Webflow template stores, itch.io, Unity Asset Store, stock sites.
- **Books:** Amazon KDP, Kobo Writing Life, Apple Books, Google Play Books, Draft2Digital, IngramSpark, Leanpub, Payhip/Gumroad.
- **Courses:** Gumroad, Payhip, Udemy, Skillshare, Teachable, Podia, Thinkific, Kajabi, Circle, Skool.
- **Audience:** YouTube, LinkedIn, X, TikTok/Reels/Shorts, Pinterest, Substack/Beehiiv/Ghost, Patreon/Ko-fi memberships, podcasts.

## Stacking order that fits a beginner
1. **Month 1:** one marketplace service + outreach + 3 case studies.
2. **Month 2:** a second offer, first pilot client, first tiny product and lead-magnet ebook.
3. **Month 3:** retainer(s), product #2, KDP or direct ebook, email list started.
4. **Months 4-6:** bundle products, mini-course, partnerships.
5. **After that:** audience monetisation, micro-SaaS experiments.

## Money flow design (target mix at month 6, an example target not a promise)
50% services (marketplace + direct) | 20% retainers | 20% products/ebooks | 10% other.
As product and retainer income grows, you can reduce time on low-paying gigs.

## Risk map
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Platform ban | Follow TOS, diversify, build an email list and own website |
| One big client leaves | Keep pipeline full; retainers; no client > 40% |
| Algorithm change | Multiple channels; direct outreach |
| Payment issues | Two payout routes; invoice promptly |
| Burnout | Fixed hours, systems, templates |
| Skill gap | Learn-by-project; deliver within your ability |
