## NOT recommended (and why)
- "Get rich quick" courses, pay-to-play jobs, crypto "bots": high scam risk.
- Mass-produced AI ebooks/low-content books: platforms reject, buyers dislike.
- Review buying, fake portfolios, impersonation, plagiarism: bans and legal risk.
- Scraping personal data or spam outreach: illegal in many places.
- Dropshipping with unsupported claims, AI "influencers" faking real people: reputational and legal risk.
- Offering regulated advice (medical, legal, financial) without qualifications.

## Idea-scoring template
Score 1-5 for: demand evidence | your proof | speed to first $ | repeatability | enjoyment | low startup cost. Do the top 2.
