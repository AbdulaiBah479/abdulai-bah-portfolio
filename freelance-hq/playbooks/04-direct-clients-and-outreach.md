# 04. Direct clients and outreach

Direct clients pay more, last longer, and have no marketplace fee. They take more effort to find. Build this alongside marketplaces.

## Where direct clients come from
1. Past marketplace clients (ask for repeat work through the platform's rules; don't breach TOS).
2. Referrals: ask every happy client "who else struggles with this?"
3. Local businesses: clinics, restaurants, shops, schools, real estate. Walk-in audits of their website, booking, WhatsApp replies, Google Maps profile.
4. Agencies needing white-label dev/design/automation capacity.
5. LinkedIn and X: show work weekly, comment with value on prospects' posts, DM after a few interactions.
6. Communities: Slack/Discord/Reddit/forums where your buyers hang out, answering questions helpfully and following self-promotion rules.
7. Cold email with a specific, researched observation.

## The lead system
`tracker/leads.csv` columns: date, source, company, contact, problem, offer, stage, next_action, next_date, value, notes.
Stages: Identified -> Contacted -> Replied -> Call booked -> Proposal -> Won/Lost.
Rule: every lead has a next action and date.

## Audit-first outreach (works for beginners)
1. Pick 20 prospects in one vertical.
2. Do a 10-minute audit each (site speed, mobile layout, missing chatbot/booking, slow replies, manual workflow). Screenshot 2 issues.
3. Send a short email: observation -> estimated impact -> one specific fix -> offer a free 15-minute walkthrough. See `templates/cold-email-sequence.md`.
4. Follow up twice, 3 and 7 days later, adding new value each time.
5. Track reply rate; adjust subject line, observation, and offer.

## Legal and ethical outreach
Use real business contact data from public pages; include your identity, a clear reason, and an easy opt-out; honour opt-outs; follow CAN-SPAM/GDPR/local rules; don't buy scraped lists of private individuals; no deception.

## Discovery call script (20 minutes)
1. Goal (2 min): "What would success look like in 90 days?"
2. Problem (8 min): current process, cost of the problem, what they've tried.
3. Constraints (3 min): budget range, deadline, decision-makers, tools.
4. Offer (5 min): outline approach and price range, next step.
5. Close (2 min): send proposal within 24h.

## Proposal to close
One page: problem, outcome, scope, deliverables, timeline, price (3 options: Good/Better/Best), terms, next step with a date. Follow up the next day, then twice more. See `templates/scope-of-work.md`.

## Retainers
After a project, offer monthly maintenance, optimisation, or content. Price on value and availability. Examples: "Automation care plan", "Chatbot tune-up and reporting", "Monthly design pack". Aim for 3 retainers before expanding.

## Systems for scale
CRM (Twenty, free/self-host, or a spreadsheet), calendar booking, invoicing, templated onboarding, review/testimonial capture, referral ask at day 30 after delivery.
