# 08. Marketing and audience

Marketing is how one skill becomes a business. Keep it simple: **proof, consistency, email list.**

## The three assets that compound
1. **Proof**: case studies, testimonials, before/after, demo videos.
2. **Audience**: posts that attract your buyers (LinkedIn, X, YouTube, TikTok/Reels, Pinterest, blog).
3. **Email list**: the only audience you own. Use Kit (ConvertKit), Beehiiv, MailerLite free tiers, or self-host **listmonk** (verify free-tier limits).

## Weekly rhythm (about 6 hours)
| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Mon | Pillar piece (case study, tutorial, or ebook chapter) |
| Tue | Repurpose into 3 posts + 1 short video script |
| Wed | Outreach: 10 personalised messages |
| Thu | Community help: answer 5 questions with value |
| Fri | Newsletter + review metrics |
| Weekend | Batch visuals (Canva) and schedule |

## Content that sells services
Before/after builds, "how I automated X for a client in 2 days", screen-recorded walkthroughs, mistakes and fixes, price breakdowns, templates you give away, myth-busting for your vertical.

## Content that sells products and ebooks
Free mini-versions, sample chapters, use-case demos, "what's inside" reels, buyer results, comparison posts ("template vs doing it from scratch"), pins linking to listings.

## Platform quick guide
- **LinkedIn**: best for B2B services; 3-4 posts a week, comment daily on prospects.
- **X/Threads/Bluesky**: dev/AI/indie audience; build in public.
- **YouTube**: slow, durable, great for tutorials; each video sells for years.
- **TikTok/Reels/Shorts**: reach; pair with a clear CTA to a free resource.
- **Pinterest**: traffic engine for printables, ebooks, templates, design.
- **Reddit/Discord/forums**: help first, follow rules, link rarely.
- **Medium/Dev.to/Hashnode**: SEO for technical writing.
- **Product Hunt / Indie Hackers / Hacker News**: launches for tools and code products.

## Lead magnet ideas
"Automation audit checklist", "AI chatbot ROI calculator", "20 prompts for [profession]", "n8n starter workflow", "Landing page teardown template". Gate with email; deliver instantly; follow with a 5-email welcome sequence (`templates/launch-email-sequence.md`).

## Email basics
One idea per email; clear subject; story -> lesson -> CTA; unsubscribe link and physical/legal identity as the law requires; send weekly. Never buy lists.

## Partnerships and affiliates
Offer creators in your niche an affiliate share (verify platform support), cross-promote with non-competing freelancers, guest on newsletters/podcasts, co-create bundles.

## SEO for your portfolio site
Use the website at the repo root (`../index.html`): add per-service pages, case studies, schema.org markup, fast loading, clear CTA, a booking link, testimonials with real names (permission). Track with Plausible/Umami (self-host or free tiers, verify) rather than invasive analytics.

## Paid ads
Not before you have a converting offer and organic proof. Start with $5-10/day tests, one platform, one offer, landing page tailored to the ad.

## Ethics
No fake social proof, no engagement pods that violate rules, no deceptive scarcity, disclose affiliates and sponsorships, respect copyright.
