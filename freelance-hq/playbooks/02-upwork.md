# 02. Upwork playbook

> Items marked `(verify)` change often. Confirm in Upwork's Help centre.

## How Upwork differs from Fiverr
Fiverr: buyers find you. Upwork: you mostly **find jobs** (and get invited once your profile ranks). Proposals cost **Connects** (verify current price per Connect and per-job amounts). Fee: **10% flat** on freelancer earnings (verify). Fixed-price contracts use milestones with escrow; hourly contracts use the work diary/time tracker for payment protection.

## Profile that converts
- Photo, specialised headline (outcome + niche), first-2-line overview hook.
- **Specialised profiles** per service (e.g. "AI chatbot developer", "n8n automation expert").
- Skills (max shown on the profile, verify), portfolio with case studies, employment/education only real.
- Project Catalog / productized listings (verify availability) for fixed-scope services.
- Hourly rate: set so you can afford to win the first jobs and raise after 3-5 reviews (see pricing cheatsheet).
- Get Job Success Score (JSS) via completed, well-reviewed contracts (verify thresholds).

## Job selection (the highest-leverage skill)
Green flags: payment verified, 4.5+ rating, hire rate above ~50%, spend history, detailed post, clear budget, recent activity.
Red flags: vague scope + big promise, "test project" unpaid, off-platform requests, many open unhired posts, requests for passwords or to install software, below-market budgets for large scope. See `cheatsheets/client-red-flags.md`.
Apply to jobs posted in the last few hours; early proposals get seen.

## Proposal anatomy (150-250 words)
1. Hook mirrors their problem (visible in the preview).
2. 3-step plan, specific to their post.
3. One relevant proof with a result.
4. One smart question.
5. CTA: short call or paid starter milestone.
Answer every screening question. Format as short paragraphs, no walls of text, no templates that start "Dear Sir".

## Pricing tactics
- Fixed-price: break into 2-3 milestones; first milestone small and cheap to reduce risk for both.
- Hourly: quote a range and cap; track time honestly.
- Raise rates every 3-5 positive contracts.

## Winning loop
Daily: 30-45 min searching with saved searches/alerts -> 3-5 targeted proposals -> follow-up on old ones -> log in `tracker/leads.csv`. Weekly: compare reply rate by proposal variant.

## After you win
Confirm scope in writing, set milestones, communicate in-platform, deliver early, request review, propose the next phase or a retainer. A great first contract becomes a repeat client; repeat clients are the real asset.

## Staying safe
Keep all communication and payment on Upwork (TOS), avoid fake-cheque or "pay for access" scams, never share passwords, use the platform for disputes. Do not buy accounts or use someone else's identity.
