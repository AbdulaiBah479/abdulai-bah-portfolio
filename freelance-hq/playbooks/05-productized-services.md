# 05. Productized services and packaging

A productized service is a service sold like a product: fixed scope, price and timeline.

## Why it wins
Buyers know exactly what they get; you can price confidently, deliver repeatably, delegate, and automate with Claude. It makes a Fiverr gig, a landing-page offer and a DM pitch the same thing.

## Build one in 7 steps
1. Pick one painful, frequent problem for one buyer type.
2. Define the **single outcome** and the minimum steps to reach it.
3. List what's **out of scope** (this protects your margin).
4. Standardise: intake form, checklist, templates, QA step, delivery doc.
5. Price from value and your target hourly rate (see pricing cheatsheet). Make 3 tiers.
6. Set the timeline you can always meet.
7. Add a guarantee you can keep (e.g. "one free revision round", never "100% satisfaction or double your money").

## Example offers
| Offer | Deliverable | Typical timeline |
|---|---|---|
| FAQ chatbot | Trained on client docs, embedded on site, handoff form | 3-7 days |
| One-workflow automation | n8n/Make flow + docs + 14-day support | 2-4 days |
| Landing page | 1 page, mobile-ready, forms, analytics | 2-5 days |
| Brand social kit | Logo usage, 12 templates, 10 posts | 5-7 days |
| Reels pack | 8 short edits with captions | 5 days |
| AI workflow audit | Report with 5 automations ranked by ROI | 2 days |

## Ladder
Free audit/lead magnet -> low-priced starter (tripwire) -> core service -> premium/implementation -> monthly retainer.

## Convert services into products
Each repeated delivery becomes a reusable asset: workflow templates, chatbot starter code, design templates, SOP checklists, prompt packs. Sell the asset on Gumroad/Payhip/Etsy while still selling the done-for-you service.

## Delivery system
Claude drafts; you review. Standard folders in `clients/`, checklists in `templates/`, quality gate `/launch-checklist`, review request after delivery.

## Metrics
Time per order, margin per order, repeat rate, refund rate, average order value, referral rate. Review monthly; raise price when the calendar is full.
