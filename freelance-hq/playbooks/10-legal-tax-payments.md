# 10. Legal, tax and payments (general information only)

**Not legal or tax advice.** Rules depend on your country and the client's. Use a local accountant/lawyer for registration, tax, contracts, and VAT/GST/sales-tax.

## Questions to answer once, with a professional
1. Do I need to register as self-employed/sole trader or a company? When?
2. What taxes apply to foreign income (marketplace, ads, ebook royalties)? Thresholds? Filing dates?
3. Do I need to charge or remit VAT/GST/sales tax on digital products I sell directly? (Merchant-of-record platforms like Lemon Squeezy/Paddle handle much of this; marketplaces like Etsy/KDP/Gumroad handle some taxes: verify per platform.)
4. Are my payment channels allowed locally (Payoneer, Wise, PayPal, crypto)? Any foreign-currency reporting?
5. What records must I keep and for how long?

## Contracts-lite essentials (`templates/contract-lite.md`)
Parties; scope and deliverables; timeline; fees and payment terms; revisions; IP (assigned on full payment, with your right to showcase work and keep pre-existing tools); confidentiality; warranties and limitation of liability; termination and kill fee; governing law; signatures (DocuSeal/Documenso self-hosted options exist, see resources).

## Intellectual property
Client owns the final deliverable after payment; you keep your tools, templates, know-how. For digital products you own the master and grant licences (personal/commercial/extended). Check third-party licences: fonts, icons, images, music, libraries, models (AI model licences and training-data claims matter).

## Data protection
Handle client data minimally; store in `clients/` (git-ignored); use NDAs when asked; do not paste client secrets or personal data into AI tools without permission and an appropriate data-handling setup; delete after the project per agreement; follow GDPR/local data laws for email lists (consent, unsubscribe, record-keeping).

## Platform rules to keep accounts alive
One account per person, honest profile, no off-platform payment, no fake reviews, no plagiarism, AI-use disclosure where required, accurate descriptions, and timely delivery.

## Marketing claims
No guaranteed income, no fake scarcity, disclose affiliates/sponsors, truthful testimonials with consent.

## Payment methods and invoices
Use platform escrow; for direct clients keep invoices (`templates/invoice.md`) with sequential numbers, dates, currency, payment terms, and tax IDs where applicable. Keep bank statements and receipts for expenses.

## Insurance and protection
Consider professional liability insurance for larger/enterprise work; back up files; use 2FA on every account; use a password manager (Bitwarden is open source).

## Disputes
Document everything in writing; try a fair partial refund or fix first; use the platform's dispute flow; for direct clients rely on your contract; avoid public arguments.
