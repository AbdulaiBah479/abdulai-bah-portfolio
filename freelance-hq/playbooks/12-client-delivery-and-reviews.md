# 12. Client delivery and reviews

Reviews and repeat clients are the compounding asset on every platform.

## The delivery pipeline
1. **Intake**: structured questions; never start vague.
2. **Scope**: deliverables, out-of-scope, assumptions, revisions, timeline, acceptance criteria.
3. **Kickoff**: restate in 5 bullets; list what you need and by when.
4. **Work in checkpoints**: share an early draft or screenshot at ~30-50% to catch misunderstandings.
5. **QA**: checklist before every delivery.
6. **Delivery**: files + short "how to use" doc + short video walkthrough if helpful.
7. **Revisions**: handle inside the revision allowance; extra scope = priced change request.
8. **Close**: confirm acceptance, ask for a review, suggest next step.
9. **Follow-up**: day 7 check-in, day 30 upsell/referral ask.

## Communication rules
Reply within the promised window; write short; one question at a time; summarise calls in writing; confirm deadlines; never go silent; bad news early with a fix.

## Scope control script
"Thanks. That's outside the current scope (X). I can add it as an extra for $Y and N days, or we can swap it for Z in the current plan. Which do you prefer?"

## Revision management
Collect feedback in one list; batch changes; confirm understanding before re-doing; limit rounds in the scope; show before/after.

## Difficult clients
- **Vague**: send options with examples; ask them to pick.
- **Slow**: set deadlines tied to your schedule; pause politely when blocked.
- **Aggressive/unreasonable**: stay factual, keep it in writing, use the platform dispute process, offer a fair partial refund when appropriate, protect your time.
- **Non-payer**: stop work, send reminders, use escrow/dispute or contract steps.

## Reviews (honest only)
Ask right after a successful delivery: "If you're happy, a review helps a lot." Don't offer incentives, don't script what they write, don't trade reviews. Reply to every review graciously. For negative reviews, respond with empathy and the fix; never argue.

## Testimonials and case studies
With permission, turn good projects into 1-page case studies: client (or anonymised), problem, solution, result. Use `templates/testimonial-request.md`. Ask for a quote and a metric.

## Turning clients into retainers/upsells
Offer a maintenance plan, optimisation sprint, analytics report, training session, or next-phase build before the project closes.

## Offboarding
Hand over files and access, document logins in the client's own password manager (never email passwords), 14-30 day support window, invoice paid in full before final transfer on direct work.

## Quality checklist (use per deliverable)
Requirements met; tested; branded; spelling; licences; no leftover client or private data; file names clear; backups; client-friendly instructions.
