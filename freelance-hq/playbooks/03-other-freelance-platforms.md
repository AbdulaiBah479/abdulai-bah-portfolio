## How to choose
1. Where do your buyers already look? (Small business owners: Fiverr/Upwork/LinkedIn. Startups: Wellfound/Braintrust. Agencies: direct outreach.)
2. Entry barrier: open (Fiverr, Upwork, Freelancer) vs vetted (Toptal, Braintrust).
3. Fee drag vs lead quality. Avoid pay-to-play platforms that charge heavy upfront fees for access.
4. Payout availability in your country. Check Payoneer, Wise, bank transfer, local options before investing time.

## Portfolio hubs (free traffic)
GitHub (pin repos with great READMEs), Behance/Dribbble (visual work), personal site (`../index.html`), LinkedIn (featured section), YouTube (walkthrough demos), Medium/Dev.to/Hashnode (technical posts that rank), Product Hunt (launching tools).

## Rules across all platforms
Same name/story everywhere, consistent niche, keep communication on-platform, never duplicate accounts on the same platform, never use fake portfolios.

See `04-direct-clients-and-outreach.md` for off-marketplace lead generation.
