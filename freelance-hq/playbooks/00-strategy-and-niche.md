# 00. Strategy and niche

## The model: three income layers
| Layer | What | Speed to first $ | Scales by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Services | Fiverr/Upwork/direct clients | Fastest (days to weeks) | Raising price, productizing, retainers |
| 2. Products | Templates, kits, code, assets | Slow start, then passive | Number of products x traffic |
| 3. Knowledge | Ebooks, guides, courses, newsletter | Slowest, compounding | Audience and email list |

**Sequence that works for a beginner:** services pay the bills and teach you what buyers want; every
repeated client request becomes a product; every product's lessons become an ebook or course; the
audience feeds back into services at higher prices.

## Pick one niche (the 30-day rule)
Do not sell "everything". Combine **skill x industry x outcome**:
- Bad: "I do AI, apps, design and automation."
- Good: "I build WhatsApp/website AI chatbots for small clinics so they stop missing bookings."

Candidate offers from the portfolio (validate with `/niche`; these are hypotheses, not facts):
1. n8n/Make automation for small agencies (lead follow-up, invoicing, reporting).
2. AI chatbot for customer support/FAQ on websites (RAG over the client's docs).
3. Landing page + simple web app builds (fast, fixed price).
4. Social-media creative and short-video editing packages (multimedia).
5. Product ideas: n8n workflow packs, prompt packs for a profession, Notion/Canva kits, chatbot starter kit.

## Validation scorecard (1-5, weight in brackets)
Demand [x3] | Competition gap [x2] | Proof you already have [x2] | Delivery hours per sale (low = good) [x2] | Repeatable/productizable [x2] | Interest [x1]. Test anything above ~75% of max.

## The 30-day test
- Define success numerically before starting (e.g. 20 proposals, 4 replies, 1 paid order; or 1 product live and 10 views/day).
- Do the work daily in the order: **profile/offer -> outreach -> delivery -> content**.
- At day 30 decide: double down / adjust one variable / kill. Record in the weekly review.

## Positioning formula
`I help [specific buyer] get [specific outcome] by [mechanism] in [timeframe], without [main fear].`

## Productized services (your fastest scalable offer)
Fixed scope, fixed price, fixed timeline, same steps every time. Examples:
- "AI FAQ chatbot on your site in 5 days, $X, includes 2 revisions and 30-day support"
- "Automate one workflow in n8n in 3 days"
- "Landing page in 48 hours"
Benefits: easy to sell, easy to price, easy to delegate/automate with Claude, easy to turn into a Fiverr gig or a product.

## Skill stacking
Rare combos win: design + automation, development + copywriting, AI + a vertical (real estate, clinics, e-commerce, education). Pick the vertical where you can get 3 case studies fastest.

## Decision rules
- If you cannot get replies: fix the offer/proposal before sending more.
- If you get replies but no sales: fix price, proof, or the call/offer.
- If you win but hate the work: raise the price or change the niche.
- Say no to jobs outside the niche until you have steady income.
