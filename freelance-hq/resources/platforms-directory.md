## Selection rules
1. Max 2 new platforms per quarter.
2. Join where buyers already spend money.
3. Check country eligibility and payouts first.
4. Avoid pay-to-play schemes and "guaranteed income" platforms.
5. Keep one master portfolio and adapt per platform.
