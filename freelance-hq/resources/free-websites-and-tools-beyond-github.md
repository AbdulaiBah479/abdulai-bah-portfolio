# Free websites and tools beyond GitHub

Free plans change. `(verify)` the current limits and licence terms on each site before relying on them or using the assets commercially.

## Research and ideas
| Need | Free options |
|---|---|
| Search interest over time | Google Trends |
| Question ideas | Google "People also ask", AnswerThePublic (free tier), AlsoAsked (free tier) |
| Keyword basics | Google Keyword Planner (needs an Ads account), marketplace search suggestions, Pinterest Trends |
| Competitors | Marketplace search pages, Wayback Machine, SimilarWeb free tier |
| Communities (read first, follow rules) | Reddit niche subs, Indie Hackers, Hacker News, Dev.to, Hashnode, Discord/Slack/Telegram groups |
| Job feeds | Upwork saved searches, LinkedIn alerts, Wellfound, We Work Remotely, Remote OK |

## Images, icons, fonts, audio (read each licence)
| Type | Options |
|---|---|
| Photos | Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay |
| Icons / illustrations | Lucide, Tabler Icons, unDraw, Heroicons |
| Fonts | Google Fonts, Fontsource |
| Music / sound | YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music, Freesound (check each file's licence) |
| Colours | Coolors, Adobe Color |
| Mockups | Free mockup packs from reputable sources (check commercial use) |
| Editing | Photopea (browser), Squoosh (compress), Remove-background tools with free tiers |
Keep a licence log in `products/<slug>/LICENSES.md`.

## Writing and checking
Hemingway-style readability tools, LanguageTool free tier, Google Docs voice typing, Zotero (references), Wikipedia/Google Scholar for fact-checking, Archive.org for open books. Never present facts without a source.

## Websites and portfolios
GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, Netlify and Vercel free tiers (verify limits and commercial-use terms), Carrd (cheap one-pagers), Linktree-style link pages, Behance/Dribbble for visuals, LinkedIn Featured section, Hashnode/Dev.to for technical posts.

## Email, forms, scheduling, signatures
- Email lists: Kit, MailerLite, Beehiiv free tiers (verify subscriber limits)
- Forms: Google Forms, Tally free tier, Formbricks
- Scheduling: Cal.com free tier, Calendly free tier, Google Calendar booking pages
- Signatures: free tiers of e-sign tools (verify), or DocuSeal/Documenso self-hosted
- Invoices: Wave (verify availability in your country), Zoho Invoice free tier (verify), `templates/invoice.md`

## Meetings and recording
Google Meet, Zoom free tier, Jitsi (open source), Loom free tier, OBS Studio. Always tell people you are recording.

## Money tools (availability depends on your country)
Wise, Payoneer, PayPal, Stripe, local mobile-money services, bank transfers. Compare fees, minimums, speed and currency conversion before choosing. Keep records of every payment.

## Learning (free)
- Anthropic documentation and the prompt tutorial
- freeCodeCamp, The Odin Project, CS50 (Harvard), Khan Academy
- Google Digital Garage, HubSpot Academy (marketing), Semrush Academy free courses (verify)
- Coursera/edX audit mode (free to watch, certificate costs)
- YouTube channels that show real accounts and numbers (be sceptical of income screenshots)
- Platform academies: Fiverr Learn, Upwork Community

## Communities for freelancers (follow each community's rules)
Reddit communities for freelancing and your trade, Indie Hackers, local meetups, university alumni groups, Telegram/WhatsApp groups for your country's freelancers. Help first; promote only where allowed.

## Safety rule for any free tool
Don't upload client or personal data to a tool whose privacy policy you haven't read. Prefer tools that let you export your data.
