# Learning resources (free first)

## Freelancing and selling
- Platform academies and help centres: Fiverr Learn / Seller help, Upwork Community & Help (primary sources for rules).
- `engineerapart/TheRemoteFreelancer` on GitHub.
- Books worth buying later: *The Freelance Bible*, *Company of One*, *The Mom Test* (validation), *Obviously Awesome* (positioning), *Building a StoryBrand* (messaging), *The Copywriter's Handbook*.
- YouTube: search "Fiverr gig SEO", "Upwork proposal teardown", "productized service" and favour creators who show real accounts and numbers.

## Skills that sell
- AI/chatbots: Anthropic docs and `anthropics/claude-cookbooks`; LangChain/LlamaIndex tutorials; RAG basics.
- Automation: n8n docs & community templates; Make academy.
- Web: freeCodeCamp, The Odin Project, web.dev.
- Design: Canva Design School, Figma tutorials, Refactoring UI (book).
- Copywriting: HubSpot Academy, Copyhackers free content.
- Video: CapCut and DaVinci Resolve official tutorials.

## Digital products & ebooks
- KDP Help pages, Draft2Digital guides, Reedsy blog (publishing craft), Joanna Penn's The Creative Penn (publishing, verify content), Etsy Seller Handbook.
- Gumroad/Payhip creator guides.

## Business basics
- Your country's government small-business/tax portal; a local accountant.
- Stripe Atlas guides & Indie Hackers for case studies.

## Learning method
One course at a time, build a deliverable from each lesson, publish it as a portfolio piece. Learn the next thing only when a paying task demands it.
