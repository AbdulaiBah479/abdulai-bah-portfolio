# Upwork cheat sheet (verify numbers on Upwork's help pages)

| Item | Value / rule |
|---|---|
| Freelancer fee | 10% flat (verify) |
| Proposal cost | Connects (verify price and per-job amounts) |
| Profile | Specialised headline, first 2 lines, portfolio, skills, specialised profiles |
| Metrics | Job Success Score, response, on-time, earnings (verify thresholds) |
| Protection | Fixed-price escrow; hourly requires work diary tracking |

## Job triage in 30 seconds
Payment verified? Client spend/hire rate? Budget realistic? Scope clear? Posted recently? Red flags? Skills match? If any critical answer is "no": skip.

## Proposal formula (HOOK)
**H**ook (mirror problem) / **O**ffer (3-step plan) / **O**ne proof / **K**ey question / CTA (call or paid starter).
Length 150-250 words. Answer all screening questions.

## First lines examples
- "You need X without Y. I've done exactly this for Z: [result]. Here's how I'd approach yours:"
- "Your post mentions [specific detail]. Before quoting, one question: ..."

## Bid strategy
Early profile: competitive but profitable rate, narrow niche, 3-5 targeted proposals a day. After reviews: raise rate; stop applying to low-budget jobs.

## Rate rules
Fixed: break into milestones, first small. Hourly: quote a range, cap hours, track time. Raise rates every 3-5 good contracts.

## Do / Don't
Do: saved searches, apply early, proof-first proposals, keep everything in-platform, follow up once.
Don't: copy-paste, bid on vague "test projects" unpaid, share contacts to avoid fees, accept requests for passwords/remote access without a safe process, take payment outside Upwork.
