# Proposal and outreach formulas

## HOOK (marketplace proposals)
**H**ook: mirror their problem in their words -> **O**ffer: 3-step plan -> **O**ne proof (with result) -> **K**ey question -> CTA.

## PAS (problem-agitate-solve)
"[Problem]. If it continues, [cost]. Here's how I'd fix it: [plan]."

## AIDA
Attention (specific observation), Interest (insight), Desire (outcome), Action (next step).

## 3-line cold email
1. Specific observation about them. 2. Impact + how you fix it (with a proof). 3. Low-friction ask ("worth a 15-minute look?").

## Follow-up cadence
Day 3: add a new tip. Day 7: short bump + case study. Day 14: break-up note ("closing the loop").

## Subject lines
"Quick idea for {Company}'s {process}", "{Name}: 2 fixes for {issue}", "Noticed {specific thing} on your site".

## Closing a proposal
"If it helps, I can start with a small paid first step: {milestone} for ${amount}. Want me to set it up?"

## Handling price objection
"I can adjust scope to fit your budget: here are two options." Never cut price without cutting scope.

## Words to avoid
"Dear Sir/Madam", "I am the best", "guaranteed", "cheap", copy-pasted skills lists, lengthy bios before the solution.
