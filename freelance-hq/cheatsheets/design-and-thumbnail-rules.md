# Design rules for thumbnails, covers and previews (Canva-friendly)

## Why this matters
People decide in under 2 seconds. Thumbnails and covers drive clicks (CTR) on Fiverr, Etsy, Gumroad, KDP and YouTube.

## Rules
1. **One idea, one focal point.** Less text, bigger text.
2. **Text:** 3-6 words max on a thumbnail. Sans-serif bold. Contrast ratio high (dark on light or light on dark).
3. **Readable at thumbnail size:** shrink your design to 150 px wide; if it's unreadable, simplify.
4. **Consistent brand:** 2 colours + 1 accent, 1-2 fonts, same layout family.
5. **Show the result:** before/after, screenshot of the output, product mockup.
6. **Faces help:** a friendly, real photo of you improves trust on service gigs (where allowed).
7. **Whitespace:** leave breathing room; avoid clutter and tiny icons.
8. **Check platform sizes** (verify): Fiverr gallery ~1280x769, KDP cover ~1600x2560, Etsy listing images ~2000 px wide, Gumroad cover 1280x720, YouTube 1280x720, LinkedIn post 1200x627.
9. **Accessible:** alt text, good contrast, no important info only in colour.
10. **Licences:** only fonts/images/icons you may use commercially; keep a license log in `products/<slug>/LICENSES.md`.

## Gig image set (3)
1. Hero: promise + visual result. 2. Process: 3 steps. 3. Proof: results/testimonial (real only) + what's included.

## Ebook cover checklist
Genre-correct style, title readable small, author name clear, subtitle small, safe margins, test beside bestsellers, export RGB for ebooks.

## Product preview set (5-7)
Cover | what's inside | mockup in use | before/after | how it works | licence & compatibility | FAQ.

## Tools
Canva (own Pro), Figma/Penpot, GIMP/Inkscape; Rembg for background removal; Upscayl for enlarging; ask Claude for layout briefs and copy.
