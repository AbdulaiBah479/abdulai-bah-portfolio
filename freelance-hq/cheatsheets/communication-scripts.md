# Communication scripts (copy, adapt to your voice)

## First reply to a new inquiry
"Hi {name}, thanks for reaching out! To make sure I understand: you need {goal} by {date}. Two quick questions: 1) {question}? 2) {question}? Once I know that, I'll send a clear scope and price. {Your name}"

## Sending a quote
"Here's what I'd do: {3 bullets}. Price: ${X} for {deliverables}, {N} days, {N} revisions. Out of scope: {list}. If you'd like to start small, I can do {mini-step} for ${Y}. Shall I set it up?"

## Price objection
"I understand budget matters. I can adjust scope to fit: Option A (core only) ${A}; Option B (core + extras) ${B}. Which fits better?"

## Extra work request (scope creep)
"Happy to help! That's outside our current scope. I can add it for ${X} and {N} extra days, or we can swap it with {item}. What do you prefer?"

## Delay (tell early)
"Quick update: I found {issue} that will delay delivery by {N} days (new date {date}). Here's what's done so far: {link}. Thanks for your patience."

## Asking for feedback
"Please list changes in one message (numbered). I'll update everything in one pass and send by {date}."

## Delivery
"All done! Files: {link}. Quick start: {3 steps}. Revisions are open until {date}. If everything is good, marking it complete helps a lot."

## Asking for a review
"I really enjoyed working on this. If you're happy, a short review would mean a lot and helps others find me. Thank you!"

## Refund / dispute (calm)
"I'm sorry this didn't meet your expectations. Here's what I propose: {fix by date} or {partial refund of X%}. I want this to be fair."

## No response chase
Day 3: "Just checking in on {X}. Do you need anything from me?" Day 7: "Pausing the project until I hear from you; ping me when you're ready." Day 14: "Closing the loop; reach out any time."

## Politely decline
"Thanks for thinking of me. This isn't a fit for my current focus ({reason}). I recommend {alternative}. Best of luck!"

## Late payment
Day 1: friendly reminder with invoice link. Day 7: "Payment is overdue; please confirm the date." Day 14: pause work per contract.

## Platform-safe rule
Keep conversations and payments on the platform. If a client asks you to leave: "I keep everything on the platform to protect both of us."
