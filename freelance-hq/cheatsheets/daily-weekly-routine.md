# Daily and weekly routine

## Daily (about 3 focused hours; scale up if full-time)
| Time | Block |
|---|---|
| 30 min | Inbox & messages (reply within 24h; faster on marketplaces) |
| 45 min | Lead gen: 5 targeted proposals / 10 outreach messages |
| 90 min | Deep work: client delivery or product/ebook building |
| 20 min | Content: post one piece (from the repurpose queue) |
| 10 min | Log trackers, plan tomorrow's top 3 |

## Weekly
Mon plan + review (`/weekly-review`) | Tue-Thu build and deliver | Wed outreach push | Fri ship something public + newsletter | Sat optional learning | Sun off.

## Monthly
Raise prices if full; refresh gigs (images, tags); update portfolio with new case study; review fees and tools; save tax set-aside; check platform policy changes; ship one product update.

## Quarterly
Pick one new lane (product line, platform, retainer); sunset one thing that underperforms; revisit niche and positioning.

## Minimum viable week (when busy)
5 proposals, 1 post, 1 hour on product/ebook, log numbers.
