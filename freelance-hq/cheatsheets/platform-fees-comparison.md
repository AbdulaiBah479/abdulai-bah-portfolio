# Platform fees comparison (approximate, last reviewed 2026-11-04: VERIFY before relying)

Always compute: `net = price - platform fee - payment processing - withdrawal/FX fees - tax`.

## Freelance marketplaces
| Platform | Typical seller fee |
|---|---|
| Fiverr | 20% |
| Upwork | 10% flat (+ Connects to bid) |
| Freelancer.com | ~10% (min fees apply) |
| PeoplePerHour | Tiered/variable |
| Contra | Commission-free for freelancers (client-side fees/paid plans exist) |
| Toptal / Braintrust | Network-managed rates |

## Product storefronts
| Platform | Typical cost | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Gumroad | ~10% + payment processing on direct sales; higher on marketplace-discovered sales | Fast setup, audience |
| Payhip | Free plan with a % fee; paid plans reduce it | Digital downloads, courses, memberships |
| Lemon Squeezy | ~5% + fixed fee, merchant of record | Global tax handling |
| Ko-fi | Free tier with shop fee; Gold reduces | Creators, tips, small shops |
| Etsy | Listing fee + transaction fee + payment processing (+ offsite ads when applicable) | Search traffic, printables, templates |
| Creative Market / Envato | Revenue share (author keeps a portion) | Design assets, themes, code |
| Shopify / own site | Plan fee + payment processing | Full control, brand |
| Stripe | ~2.9% + fixed fee typical | Own checkout |

## Ebook retailers
| Retailer | Typical author share |
|---|---|
| Amazon KDP ebook | 70% (price band) or 35% |
| Kobo Writing Life | ~70% (price band) |
| Apple Books | ~70% |
| Google Play Books | ~70% |
| Draft2Digital | Retailer share minus ~10% |
| Direct (Payhip/Gumroad) | Higher margin, you own customers |

Treat all numbers as starting points; fee pages change, regional variations apply.
