# Prompt library (copy, fill the brackets)

## Strategy
- Niche test: "Using my skills [..] and goal [..], propose 5 service/product/ebook ideas. For each: buyer, painful problem, evidence to check, price range, effort per sale. Rank and recommend one 30-day test with a numeric success threshold."
- Competitor teardown: "Here are 5 competing gigs/products [paste]. Extract promises, price tiers, weaknesses from reviews, and a differentiated offer I can build."

## Fiverr
- Gig builder: "Create a Fiverr gig for [service] targeting [buyer]. Title options (80 chars), 3 packages with prices and delivery, a 1,200-character description, FAQ, requirements questions, 5 tags, and 3 gallery image concepts."
- Buyer request reply: "Write a 90-word reply to this buyer request [paste] using my proof [..]. Mirror their goal, give one idea, price, delivery, and a question."

## Upwork
- Proposal: "Triage this job [paste] (score + red flags). If worth it, write a 200-word HOOK proposal using my relevant project [..] with a starter-milestone option."

## Client work
- Intake: "Make a 10-question intake form for [service] that prevents rework."
- Scope: "Draft a scope of work for [project]: deliverables, out-of-scope, assumptions, timeline, revisions, acceptance criteria."
- Update: "Write a 120-word progress update: done, next, risks, what I need from the client."
- Difficult message: "Rewrite this message to be firm, kind, and scope-protecting: [paste]."

## Products
- Idea: "Suggest 10 digital products for [audience] from these client problems [..]. Score demand, effort, and price."
- Listing: "Write an Etsy/Gumroad listing for [product]: title, 5 benefits, description, FAQ, tags, licence."

## Ebooks
- Outline: "Create a chapter outline for a [pages]-page guide helping [reader] achieve [result]. One takeaway and one exercise per chapter."
- Interview: "Interview me with 15 questions to extract my real experience for chapter [..]; then draft the chapter in my voice using only my answers."
- Edit: "Edit for clarity and concision; keep my voice; flag unsupported claims instead of inventing evidence."

## Marketing
- Repurpose: "Turn this case study [paste] into a LinkedIn post, carousel outline, 3 short posts, a 45-second video script, and a newsletter."
- Email sequence: "Write a 5-email welcome sequence for [lead magnet] that ends with an offer for [product/service]."

## Finance
- Weekly review: "Here is my income.csv and leads.csv [paste]. Diagnose bottlenecks and propose 3 experiments for next week."

## Safety clause to append when needed
"Do not invent statistics, quotes or testimonials. Mark assumptions. Flag anything I must verify."
