# Digital product launch cheat sheet

## Idea filter (need 4 of 5)
Seen selling | Painful problem | Solved in 15 min by your product | Differentiator | Cheap to ship v1.

## MVP definition
One job. One result. One format. One price. A guide. A licence.

## Listing checklist
- [ ] Title: benefit + type (e.g. "AI Chatbot Starter Kit for Small Business Websites")
- [ ] 5-7 images: cover, inside, mockup, before/after, license, FAQ
- [ ] Description: problem -> solution -> inside -> for whom -> how -> licence -> FAQ
- [ ] Tags/keywords researched
- [ ] Price + launch discount + end date
- [ ] Preview/demo, sample download
- [ ] Refund & support policy
- [ ] Email capture connected

## 14-day launch plan
-7 waitlist | -3 proof | 0 launch email + posts | 1-7 daily use-case posts, reply to all | 7 deadline reminder | 14 review data, ship v1.1.

## Metrics to watch
Views, conversion, refunds, revenue/visitor, emails captured, reviews.

## Upsell ideas
Bundle, extended licence, done-for-you setup, course, monthly updates.

## Risks
Copyright/licence issues, leftover client data, over-promising, platform rule violations, unreadable thumbnails, no traffic plan.
