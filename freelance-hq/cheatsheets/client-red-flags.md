# Client red flags and scam checklist

## Hard stops (decline or report)
- Overpayment / fake cheque / "send back the difference"
- Asks you to pay for access, a "training kit", or a "license" to get the job
- Wants to move chat/payment off-platform immediately
- Asks you to install unknown software, remote-control your PC, or share passwords/2FA
- Asks for your ID/bank details before any contract on a non-verified platform
- Requests illegal work, plagiarism, fake reviews, academic cheating, impersonation
- "Test project" that is actually the real deliverable, unpaid
- Job post copies your profile text or contacts you with a generic wall of praise and a link

## Soft flags (proceed carefully, price up, shrink scope, deposit)
- Vague scope with huge promises
- Unrealistic budget/timeline
- Many open unhired posts, bad ratings from freelancers
- Disrespect in first messages, pressure tactics
- "Equity instead of payment"/exposure only
- Unclear decision-maker
- Changes scope before starting

## Verification steps
Payment verified; client's company real (website, LinkedIn); read history/reviews; ask clarifying questions; use milestones; deposit for direct work; sign scope; no deliverable before payment unless low-risk.

## If scammed
Stop contact, report to the platform, preserve evidence, don't send money, change passwords, enable 2FA.
