# Five Quick Wins to Make Your Small Business Easier to Find Online

Most customers search before they buy. If your business is hard to find, they simply choose someone else. These five fixes take about an hour each.

## 1. Claim your Google Business Profile

Add your opening hours, phone number, address and three clear photos. Read [Google's guide to Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business) for the steps.

![Owner updating opening hours on a phone](images/profile-hours.jpg)

## 2. Add a WhatsApp or contact button

Make it one tap for a customer to reach you.

## 3. Ask happy customers for a review

Send the link right after a good experience.
