# One-page offer sheet (design in Canva; use for WhatsApp, print and email)

## Layout (A4 / mobile-friendly)
1. **Headline (result):** "Get found, answer customers and save hours every week."
2. **Subline:** "Bah.Digital helps small businesses in <place> with websites, WhatsApp, AI assistants and automation."
3. **Photo of you + 1-line credibility** (real only): "Multimedia & Technology student and builder of a farmer marketplace, an AI chatbot and an automation pipeline."
4. **3 offers (cards):**
   - **Free 15-min Digital Check:** 3 quick wins for your business.
   - **AI Business Audit ($50-300):** where AI and automation save you time and money, with a 90-day plan.
   - **Get Found / Online in a Week:** Google profile + WhatsApp setup + landing page.
5. **Proof strip:** 3 mini case studies (real). Add a real testimonial when you have one.
6. **How it works:** 1) Free check 2) Pick a package 3) 50% deposit 4) Delivery 5) Monthly support.
7. **Guarantee you can keep:** "Free fixes for 14 days after delivery."
8. **Contact:** WhatsApp number, email, website, social handles. QR code to WhatsApp chat.
9. **Small print:** payment methods, revisions, timeline.

## Text version (for WhatsApp)
```
Hi <name>, I'm Abdulai from Bah.Digital.
I help <business type> with:
1) A free 15-minute Digital Check (3 quick wins)
2) Getting found on Google + WhatsApp set up properly
3) A simple website/landing page
4) AI assistant + automation that answers customers and saves hours

I can send a free mini-check of <their business> today. Interested?
```

## Design rules
Max 2 colours + 1 accent, big readable text, one clear action (WhatsApp me), real photos, no stock-photo lies. Use Brand Kit in Canva. Test on a phone screen.
