# Client intake questionnaire

1. What outcome would make this project a success in 90 days? (metric if possible)
2. Who is the target audience/user?
3. What have you already tried or built? Links and access (read-only where possible).
4. 2-3 examples you like and what you like about them.
5. Brand assets: logo, colours, fonts, tone, existing content (send via the platform).
6. Tools/platforms involved (CRM, website platform, hosting, messaging channels).
7. Must-haves vs nice-to-haves.
8. Deadline and why it matters.
9. Who approves the work? How quickly can you give feedback?
10. Budget range and preferred payment structure.
11. Anything I should avoid (legal, brand, technical constraints)?
12. Data or accounts I'll touch, and any confidentiality requirements.
