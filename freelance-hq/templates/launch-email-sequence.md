# Launch and welcome email sequences (send only to people who opted in; include unsubscribe link and sender identity)

## Welcome sequence (5 emails after lead magnet)
1. Day 0: Deliver the freebie + one quick win + what to expect.
2. Day 2: Story: the problem you solved and the lesson.
3. Day 4: Tutorial: one useful tactic with steps.
4. Day 6: Case study with real numbers (permission) + soft CTA.
5. Day 8: Offer: your product/service, who it's for, guarantee/refund, deadline for a welcome discount.

## Product launch (3-5 emails)
- T-3: Teaser: "Something's coming" + waitlist benefit.
- T0: Launch: what it is, who for, price, launch discount ends {date}, link.
- T+2: Proof: use-case or customer result.
- T+5: FAQ + objections.
- T+7: Last call: deadline today.

## Subject line patterns
"The {X} I wish I had when {situation}", "How I {result} in {time}", "{Product}: {discount} ends tonight".

## Rules
Honest scarcity only; plain text works; one CTA; test on mobile; keep unsubscribes working; comply with CAN-SPAM/GDPR.
