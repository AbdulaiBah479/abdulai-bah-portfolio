# WhatsApp and DM sales messages (local and international)

Use only with businesses where contacting them is appropriate (their public business number or page), keep it short, be polite, and stop if they say no.

## 1. First message (specific observation)
"Hello <name>, I'm Abdulai from Bah.Digital. I looked at <business> on <Google Maps/Facebook> and noticed <specific thing, e.g. customers can't find your opening hours / no booking link>. I can fix this quickly. May I send you a free mini-check with 3 improvements? No obligation."

## 2. If they reply "yes"
"Great, thank you! Here is your mini-check: 1) ... 2) ... 3) ... If you'd like, I can do #1 for $<price> this week. Should I go ahead?"

## 3. If they ask the price
"It depends on what you need. Most owners start with <package> at $<price> (50% to begin, balance on delivery). I can do a smaller version for $<price> if the budget is tight. Which fits better?"

## 4. If they say "too expensive"
"I understand. Option A (core only) is $<A>. Option B (more) is $<B>. We can also start with just <smallest step> for $<C>."

## 5. If they say "I'll think about it"
"Of course. I'll send you a one-page summary. If it helps, I'm free for a 10-minute call <day>. I'll check back on <date>."

## 6. Follow-up (3 days later)
"Hi <name>, quick follow-up. One idea that might help <business>: <tip>. Happy to help if you'd like."

## 7. Booking the work
"Perfect. To start I need: 1) <asset> 2) <access/info> 3) 50% deposit via <Orange Money/bank/Wise> to <details>. I'll send you a short scope and a receipt."

## 8. Delivery
"Done! Here's everything: <link>. How to use it: <3 steps>. You have 14 days of free fixes. If you're happy, would you write one honest sentence I can show other businesses?"

## 9. Monthly plan after delivery
"To keep it working I offer a monthly care plan from $<price>: updates, backups and a monthly report. First month free if you start today?" (Only if you can honour it.)

## 10. Referral ask
"Thank you! Do you know another business that needs this? I'll give them and you a 10% discount."

## 11. International DM (LinkedIn/email)
"Hi <name>, I noticed <observation>. I run Bah.Digital and help small businesses with <outcome>. Worth a quick 15-minute look? I can also send a one-page teardown first. If not relevant, no worries and I won't follow up."

## 12. Opt-out and politeness
If someone says no or asks you to stop: "Understood, thank you. I won't contact you again." Never send bulk or repeated messages.

## Rules
Be truthful about experience (you are building your portfolio), never claim clients you do not have, never promise results you cannot control, and keep payment records.
