# Cold outreach sequence (lawful, personalised, opt-out honoured)

**Before sending:** research the prospect, one specific observation, real sender identity, easy opt-out ("reply 'no thanks' and I won't contact you again").

## Email 1 (Day 0)
Subject: Quick idea for {Company}'s {process}
Hi {Name}, I noticed {specific observation}. That usually costs {impact}. A fix I've used for {similar client/proof}: {one-line fix}. Worth a 15-minute look? I can send a 1-page teardown first. {Name}

## Email 2 (Day 3)
Subject: Re: Quick idea for {Company}'s {process}
Adding one thing: {tip or screenshot}. Happy to share the teardown if it helps.

## Email 3 (Day 7)
Subject: {Case study title}
Short case study: {problem -> action -> result, 3 lines + link}. Open to a quick call this week?

## Email 4 (Day 14, break-up)
I'll close the loop here. If {problem} becomes a priority, I'm happy to help. Wishing you a great quarter.

## Metrics
Sent / opens (if tracked ethically) / replies / calls / wins. Improve the observation and offer before sending more volume.
