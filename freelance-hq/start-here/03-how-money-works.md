# 03. How the money actually works (with maths)

The numbers below are **worked examples to teach you the maths, not predictions**. Real results vary a lot.

## 1. The funnel: every income source is the same machine
```
People who SEE your offer  ->  CLICK  ->  CONTACT you  ->  BUY  ->  BUY AGAIN / REFER
```
Each step loses people. Income = (people at the top) x (conversion at each step) x (price) x (repeat factor). To earn more you improve ONE step at a time:
- Few people see you -> improve keywords, post more, apply to more jobs.
- Seen but not clicked -> better title and thumbnail.
- Clicked but no message -> clearer offer, proof, price.
- Messages but no sales -> better reply, call, proposal, guarantee.
- Sales but no repeat -> better delivery, follow-up, retainers.

## 2. Freelance example (proposal route)
Assume you send **100 proposals**, **10%** get a reply, **30%** of replies become jobs, average job **$150**:
`100 x 0.10 x 0.30 = 3 jobs = $450 gross`.
After a 10-20% platform cut and costs, maybe ~$370 net. If proposals cost Connects (~$0.15 each, verify), 100 proposals with ~8 Connects each is about $120 of Connects: that is why **quality beats volume**. Improving reply rate from 10% to 20% doubles income without extra cost.

## 3. Fiverr example (gig route)
Assume a gig gets **2,000 impressions** in a month, **2% click** (40 clicks), **5% of clicks order** (2 orders) at $80: `2 orders = $160 gross, $128 net after 20%`.
To improve: more impressions (keywords), higher CTR (thumbnail), better conversion (reviews, FAQ). Reviews compound: the 10th order is easier than the 1st.

## 4. Digital product example
Product price $19. Platform fee ~10% plus payment fees: net around $16.
- 500 visitors/month x 2% buy = 10 sales = $160 net.
- To reach $1,000/month you need ~63 sales: more traffic, a bundle at $49, or several products.
Products reward **traffic** (SEO, email, social) and **number of products**. One product alone rarely does much; ten good ones can.

## 5. Ebook example
Ebook at $4.99 on KDP: 70% royalty ~ $3.49 minus a small delivery fee (verify). 100 sales/month = ~$349. Selling the same ebook directly at $9 keeps ~90% (~$8): 40 direct sales = $320. **Your own email list makes direct sales possible.** Free ebooks (lead magnets) pay by building the list that buys other things.

## 6. The ladder (how beginners grow income)
| Stage | Typical situation | Main goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | No reviews, no list | First 3 paid jobs and 3 honest reviews |
| 1 | A few reviews | Raise prices 20-30%, niche down |
| 2 | Repeat clients | Retainers, productized service |
| 3 | Time is full | Turn repeated work into products and an ebook |
| 4 | Audience exists | Courses, bundles, partnerships |

## 7. Your real hourly rate
`effective rate = net income / ALL hours` (including searching, messaging, fixing, admin). New freelancers often earn little per hour at first because setup and learning take time. Judge progress by **leading numbers** (proposals sent, reply rate, gig clicks, emails collected) in the first 30-60 days, not just income.

## 8. Costs to remember
Platform fees, payment/withdrawal fees, currency conversion, software (aim for free), your internet, taxes, time. Use `python3 scripts/price_calc.py` (see below) to know the real price to charge.

## 9. Cash-flow reality
Marketplaces often hold funds for a clearing period after delivery (days to weeks). Plan for money arriving later than work is done. Keep a small buffer and never spend money you have not received.

## 10. Why diversify
If 90% of income comes from one platform or one client, one ban or lost client wipes you out. Aim for no source above 50%. That is the reason this project covers freelance + products + ebooks + audience.
