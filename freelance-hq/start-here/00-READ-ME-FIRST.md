# READ ME FIRST (for complete beginners)

You do not need to know anything about freelancing, selling online, or publishing to use this project. This page explains the whole idea in plain language. Read it once, then follow `04-first-14-days.md`.

## What is this project?
It is a **folder of instructions and tools for an AI assistant (Claude)** so that Claude can help you build a small online business. Think of it as hiring a very fast assistant and giving them a company handbook. The handbook is this folder.

You are still the boss and the face of the business. Claude writes drafts, does research, builds files and keeps you organised. **You** create accounts, approve everything, click Publish, talk to real people, and take responsibility for the quality.

## The business in one picture
There are four ways to earn money online with skills you can learn. This project covers all of them:

```
        1. SELL YOUR TIME/SKILL          2. SELL THINGS YOU MAKE ONCE
        (freelancing)                     (digital products)
        Fiverr, Upwork, direct clients    templates, kits, code, designs
        Fast first money, limited by      Slow start, then can sell while
        your hours                        you sleep
                    \                      /
                     \                    /
                      3. SELL WHAT YOU KNOW          4. BUILD AN AUDIENCE
                      (ebooks, guides, courses)      (posts, email list, videos)
                      Turns experience into          Makes 1-3 easier and cheaper
                      a product                      over time
```

**How they connect:** freelancing pays the bills now and teaches you what people will pay for. The jobs you repeat become digital products. What you learn becomes an ebook or course. An audience makes all of it easier. Start with 1, add the others slowly.

## Honest expectations (please read)
- **Nobody can promise you income.** Be suspicious of anyone who does, including courses that say "$10k/month with AI in 30 days".
- **The first sale is the hardest.** On Fiverr and Upwork you start with no reviews. Many beginners need weeks, sometimes a few months, to get steady orders. That is normal, not a sign you are bad at this.
- **Most of the work is not the skill; it is being clear and visible.** A clear offer, good proof, fast replies and many honest attempts beat raw talent.
- **AI makes you faster, not automatic.** Buyers pay for results and trust, not for "AI wrote it". Always check Claude's work.
- **Rules matter.** Platforms ban accounts for fake reviews, copied work, hidden AI use where disclosure is required, and taking payment off-platform. Banned accounts lose their money and reputation. This project is built to keep you safe.
- **Money rules depend on your country.** Taxes, getting paid, and which platforms accept you vary. Check your own country's rules and talk to a local accountant.

## The three golden rules
1. **One thing at a time.** Pick ONE service to sell for the first 30 days.
2. **Show real proof.** Only real work, real results, real reviews.
3. **Track everything.** What you do, what happens, what you change. This is how beginners become professionals.
