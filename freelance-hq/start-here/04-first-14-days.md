# 04. Your first 14 days (no experience assumed)

Plan for about **2-3 hours a day**. If you have less, spread it over more days; the order matters more than speed. Each day has: **Goal**, **Do**, **Ask Claude**, **Done when**. Tick things off in `my-plan.md`.

## Day 1: Set up and get clear
- **Goal:** working tools and a direction.
- **Do:** follow `01-setup-windows.md`. Turn on 2FA for email and GitHub.
- **Ask Claude:** `/start` (it interviews you and writes `my-plan.md`).
- **Done when:** `claude` starts in `freelance-hq/` and `my-plan.md` exists.

## Day 2: Choose ONE offer
- **Goal:** one clear thing to sell.
- **Do:** read `offers/00-offers-overview.md`; look at the top 3 Claude suggests.
- **Ask Claude:** `/niche` then "Compare the top 3 for a beginner with my portfolio. Which one is fastest to a first paid order and why?"
- **Done when:** you wrote one sentence: "I help [who] get [result] by [how]."

## Day 3: Build proof from your portfolio
- **Goal:** 3 mini case studies (problem -> what you built -> result).
- **Ask Claude:** `/case-study` for each of your portfolio projects (Farmer Marketplace, Smart Automation Pipeline, AI Chatbot System).
- **Done when:** 3 files in `clients/_self/case-studies/`. Edit them so every claim is TRUE.

## Day 4: Make the sales assets
- **Goal:** your photo, bio, gig text.
- **Do:** a clear friendly photo (face visible, good light, plain background).
- **Ask Claude:** "Use platform-profile-optimizer to write my Fiverr profile and Upwork profile" and `/gig <your offer>`.
- **Done when:** draft profile text and one full gig draft exist.

## Day 5: Create your accounts (you do this by hand)
- **Do:** read `05-account-setup.md`. Create Fiverr and Upwork accounts, plus a payout method (check your country's options first). Use your real name and details; one account per platform.
- **Done when:** both profiles are filled in (publish the gig only after the Day 6 review).

## Day 6: Review and publish your first gig
- **Ask Claude:** `/launch-checklist gig`. Fix every item.
- **Do:** create images with Canva (ask Claude for the layout brief), paste text into Fiverr, publish.
- **Done when:** gig is live. Add a row to `tracker/gigs.csv`.

## Day 7: Rest, review, learn
- **Do:** read `playbooks/01-fiverr.md` and `cheatsheets/fiverr.md`. Watch how top gigs in your category look.
- **Ask Claude:** `/weekly-review` (it will be short this week).

## Day 8-10: Send proposals
- **Do:** each day find 5 fitting jobs (Upwork saved search for your offer's keywords). Skip any that fail the red-flag check.
- **Ask Claude:** `/proposal <paste the job>` for each. Edit to sound like you. Send. Log in `tracker/leads.csv`.
- **Done when:** 15 quality proposals sent. (Ten great beat fifty generic.)

## Day 11: Direct outreach (the free-lead route)
- **Do:** pick 20 local businesses (clinics, shops, schools, agencies) in one category.
- **Ask Claude:** `/audit <business website or name>` for 5 of them, then `playbooks/04` for the email.
- **Done when:** 5 audits done and 5 polite emails sent (real addresses from their public pages; include opt-out).

## Day 12: Start the asset layer
- **Ask Claude:** `/idea` to list product ideas from what you have already built; pick one tiny product (a checklist, template or workflow). Start `/product`.
- **Done when:** the product has a written promise and an outline.

## Day 13: Start a lead magnet ebook
- **Ask Claude:** `/ebook "10 things small businesses should automate first"` (or your topic). Draft the outline and interview yourself: Claude will ask questions.
- **Done when:** outline plus chapter 1 draft in `ebooks/<slug>/manuscript/`.

## Day 14: Review and decide
- **Ask Claude:** `/weekly-review`. Look at: proposals sent, replies, gig impressions/clicks, outreach replies.
- **Decide with Claude:** keep the offer, change one thing, or switch. Plan days 15-30.

## If no one replies in 14 days
This is common. Do NOT conclude you cannot do it. Diagnose:
- No views on gig -> keywords/category wrong.
- Views but no clicks -> weak thumbnail/title.
- Clicks but no messages -> offer unclear, price too high/low, no proof.
- Proposals ignored -> too generic, wrong jobs, no relevant proof.
Change ONE thing, wait a few days, measure again.

## Daily mini-routine (after day 14)
1. Check messages (15 min). 2. Five proposals/outreach (45 min). 3. Build work (90 min). 4. Post one piece of content (20 min). 5. Update trackers (10 min).
