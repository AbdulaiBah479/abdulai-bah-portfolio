# 02. Glossary (plain-language, A to Z)

Ask Claude `/explain <word>` for anything missing.

## Freelancing words
- **Freelancer**: someone who sells their skills to different clients without being an employee.
- **Client / buyer**: the person paying you.
- **Gig** (Fiverr): a fixed service you list for sale, like a shop product ("I will build your chatbot").
- **Package / tier**: Basic, Standard, Premium versions of a gig at different prices.
- **Proposal / bid**: your message answering a job post (Upwork etc.) saying why you are right and what it costs.
- **Connects** (Upwork): paid tokens used to send proposals.
- **Escrow**: the platform holds the client's money safely until you deliver. Protects both sides.
- **Milestone**: one stage of a bigger job with its own payment.
- **Scope**: exactly what is included in the job. **Scope creep**: the client keeps asking for extra things for free.
- **Out of scope**: things you agreed NOT to do.
- **Revision**: a round of changes after delivery.
- **Deliverable**: the thing you hand over (website, logo, file).
- **Retainer**: a client pays a fixed monthly amount for ongoing help.
- **Productized service**: a service with fixed scope, price and time, sold like a product.
- **White-label**: you do work that another business sells under its own name.
- **Niche**: a narrow focus (e.g. "chatbots for dental clinics").
- **Portfolio**: examples of your work. **Case study**: a portfolio item that tells problem -> what you did -> result.
- **Testimonial / review**: what a happy client says about you.
- **Level / badge**: platform status that boosts trust and ranking (e.g. Fiverr levels).
- **Buyer request**: a Fiverr feature where buyers post what they need and sellers answer (availability varies).

## Money words
- **Gross**: money before fees. **Net**: money in your pocket after fees. **Fee/commission**: the platform's cut.
- **Invoice**: a bill you send a client. **Deposit**: part paid upfront.
- **Payout / withdrawal**: moving your earnings to your bank/wallet.
- **Payoneer / Wise / PayPal / Stripe**: services that move money internationally or accept payments.
- **Merchant of record (MoR)**: a platform that legally sells your product on your behalf and handles sales tax (e.g. Lemon Squeezy).
- **VAT / GST / sales tax**: taxes added to sales in many countries.
- **Effective hourly rate**: net income divided by ALL hours worked (including finding clients).
- **AOV (average order value)**: average amount per order.
- **Passive income**: money from assets that sell without your time per sale (still needs setup and marketing).
- **Royalty**: the share of a sale you get as an author.

## Marketing words
- **SEO**: making your listing easy to find in searches. **Keyword**: a phrase people type.
- **Impression**: your listing was shown. **Click**: someone opened it. **CTR**: clicks divided by impressions.
- **Conversion rate**: the percentage of visitors who buy.
- **Funnel**: the steps from stranger to buyer (see -> click -> read -> buy).
- **Lead**: a potential customer. **Lead magnet**: a free useful thing given in exchange for an email address.
- **Email list / newsletter**: people who gave permission to receive your emails.
- **CTA (call to action)**: the one thing you ask the reader to do next.
- **Affiliate**: you earn a commission by recommending someone else's product.
- **Cold outreach**: contacting someone who does not know you yet (done politely and lawfully).
- **Social proof**: evidence others trust you (reviews, numbers, logos).
- **Hook**: the first line that earns attention.
- **Repurpose**: turning one piece of content into many.

## Digital product / ebook words
- **Digital product**: a file or access delivered online (template, course, ebook, code).
- **Template**: a ready-made file the buyer customises.
- **Licence**: the rules for how a buyer may use the product (personal vs commercial).
- **Bundle**: several products sold together.
- **Ebook / EPUB / PDF / Kindle**: digital book formats. EPUB works on most e-readers; KDP is Amazon's self-publishing service.
- **ISBN**: a book ID number (optional for some ebooks).
- **Manuscript**: your book text. **Metadata**: title, description, keywords, categories.
- **Print on demand (POD)**: items printed only when someone orders.
- **KDP Select**: an Amazon program that requires selling the ebook only on Amazon for 90 days in exchange for extra promotion tools.
- **Wide**: selling an ebook on many stores.

## AI and tech words
- **AI model / LLM**: the "brain" (like Claude) that reads and writes text.
- **Prompt**: the instruction you give an AI.
- **Context**: everything the AI can see right now.
- **Agent**: an AI that plans steps and uses tools to complete a task.
- **Subagent**: a specialist helper that Claude can hand part of a job to (this project has 11).
- **Skill**: a saved how-to that Claude loads when relevant (like a recipe card).
- **Slash command**: a shortcut you type (`/gig`) to start a prepared workflow.
- **CLAUDE.md**: the project handbook Claude reads at the start.
- **MCP (Model Context Protocol)**: a standard plug that lets Claude connect to apps (Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, Canva...).
- **Connector**: an MCP plug you switch on.
- **Chatbot**: software that talks with users. **RAG**: giving a chatbot your documents so it answers from them.
- **Automation / workflow**: steps that run on their own (new form entry -> add to spreadsheet -> send email).
- **n8n / Make / Zapier**: tools to build automations by connecting boxes.
- **API**: a way for programs to talk to each other. **Webhook**: an automatic "something happened" message between apps.
- **Open source**: software whose code is public; check its **licence** before commercial use.
- **Repository (repo)**: a project folder stored with Git/GitHub. **Clone**: download a repo. **Commit**: save a version. **Push**: upload commits. **Branch**: a separate line of work.
- **Terminal / CLI**: typing commands instead of clicking.
- **Markdown (.md)**: simple formatted text (`#` for headings). **CSV**: a spreadsheet saved as plain text.
- **Docker**: packages software so it runs the same everywhere.
- **Self-host**: run a tool on your own computer/server rather than paying for the company's version.

## Safety words
- **Scam / red flag**: sign of a dishonest client (overpay and refund, off-platform payment).
- **TOS (Terms of Service)**: the platform's rules you agreed to.
- **NDA**: a confidentiality agreement.
- **IP (intellectual property)**: who owns the work.
- **GDPR / data protection**: laws about handling people's personal data.
- **2FA**: a second login step (code on your phone). Turn it on everywhere.
