# 07. Beginner mistakes and how to avoid them

| # | Mistake | Why it hurts | Do this instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Selling "everything" | Nobody knows what to buy from you | One niche for 30 days |
| 2 | Waiting until perfect | You never launch | Ship a good version, improve with feedback |
| 3 | Copying other people's gigs/profiles | Banned, and you look the same as everyone | Study, then write from your real work |
| 4 | Generic copy-paste proposals | Ignored | Mirror their problem, one proof, one question |
| 5 | Underpricing to "get started" forever | Burnout, attracts bad clients | Low only for the first 3-5 reviews, with a plan to raise |
| 6 | Overpromising speed/results | Bad reviews | Promise what you can deliver with a buffer |
| 7 | Skipping scope and revisions | Free work forever | Write out-of-scope and revision limits |
| 8 | Taking payment/chat off-platform | Account ban, no protection | Stay in platform |
| 9 | Fake reviews, fake portfolios | Permanent ban | Only real work; do free/low-cost real pilot projects instead |
| 10 | Using AI output unchecked | Errors, plagiarism, mismatch | Review everything; disclose where required |
| 11 | Ignoring red flags | Scams, non-payment | Use `cheatsheets/client-red-flags.md` |
| 12 | Not tracking numbers | Can't tell what works | Update trackers daily |
| 13 | Building 10 products with no audience | No buyers | Validate first; build email list |
| 14 | Publishing ebooks as low-effort AI filler | Rejected, bad reviews, bans | Real experience, original value, honest disclosure |
| 15 | Ignoring taxes and rules | Trouble later | Save a % of every payment; ask a local accountant |
| 16 | Not learning from each lost proposal | Same results | Weekly review, change one variable |
| 17 | Doing everything alone forever | Slow | Use templates, automate, partner with agencies |
| 18 | Quitting at day 20 | The compounding hasn't started | Commit to 90 days with numeric goals |
| 19 | Comparing to screenshots of others' income | Discouragement, bad decisions | Compare only to your last month |
| 20 | Neglecting rest | Burnout | Fixed working hours, one day off |

## Common emotions (normal)
- "Nobody is replying": Improve one thing and keep volume steady.
- "Other freelancers are better": Narrow your niche; you don't have to be best at everything, only for one buyer.
- "Imposter syndrome": Deliver small proven wins. Confidence follows evidence.
- "I'm overwhelmed": Do the minimum viable week (5 proposals, 1 post, 1 hour on a product).
