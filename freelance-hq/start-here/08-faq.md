# 08. FAQ

**Do I need a degree or certificates?** No. Buyers care about proof of results. Certificates help only a little; a portfolio helps a lot.

**Do I need money to start?** You can start at $0: free accounts, free tools. Possible later costs: Upwork Connects, a domain, a paid design tool. Ask before spending.

**Is it okay to use AI for client work?** Often yes, but follow each platform's and client's rules, disclose when required, review everything, never claim human-only work if it is not, and never feed confidential client data to tools you don't control.

**Can Claude do the whole job for me?** Claude drafts, researches, builds and organizes. You supply judgement, real experience, approvals and relationships. Buyers pay for your accountability.

**What if I don't know how to do a service I list?** Only list services you can deliver. Learn first using projects (your portfolio already covers chatbots, automation, apps, design). Start with the smallest version you can do reliably.

**Which is better, Fiverr or Upwork?** Fiverr: buyers find you, good for productized services. Upwork: you hunt for jobs, good for longer work and higher budgets. Do both for 30 days, then double down on what responds.

**How many gigs should I make?** 2-3 narrow ones, not 15.

**How do I get my first review?** Deliver a small order excellently and ask politely. Do paid pilot projects. Never buy or swap reviews.

**What if a client is rude or won't pay?** Stay calm, document in writing, use platform dispute tools, offer a fair fix if you made a mistake, and use red-flag checks to avoid the next one.

**Can I sell the same thing on many platforms?** Yes for products and ebooks (check exclusivity rules, e.g. KDP Select). For services, keep one account per platform.

**How do digital products and ebooks make money if I'm new?** They need traffic. Build traffic through marketplace search, free lead magnets, content, and by selling to your freelance clients.

**How long until I earn?** Unknown. Some get orders in weeks, others take months. Judge yourself by activity metrics and improvement trends first.

**Is this legal where I live?** Usually yes, but taxes, registration and payment rules differ. Check your country's government portal and ask a local accountant.

**Where do I get help?** Ask Claude (`/explain`), platform help centres, freeCodeCamp forum, relevant subreddits (follow rules), and communities on Discord.

**What do I do when a rule here says (verify)?** Open the platform's official help page and confirm the number. If it differs, tell Claude to update the file.

**Should I merge this into my main repo?** Optional. Keep client data out of Git either way (already configured).
