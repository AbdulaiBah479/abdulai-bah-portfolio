# Offers overview: what you can actually sell

An **offer** is a specific thing a specific buyer pays for. Beginners fail by being vague ("I do AI"). Winners are specific ("I set up an AI chatbot on your website that answers customer questions 24/7, in 5 days").

Details for every offer are in `01-offers-catalog.md`. Price ranges there are **starting points to test, not guarantees**; check what real competitors charge with `/niche`.

## How to choose (decision tree)
1. Which offers can you deliver *well* within a week today? (Be honest.) Cross out the rest.
2. Which have proof in your portfolio? Prefer those.
3. Which have buyers on Fiverr/Upwork *right now*? (`/niche` checks live demand.)
4. Pick TWO: one **fast-cash** (e.g. #1 or #2) and one **asset-builder** (e.g. #3 or #6) that turns into a product later.
5. Test for 30 days. Keep, tweak, or swap.

## Offer ladder (how they stack)
`Free audit` -> `$ starter (small paid pilot)` -> `core project` -> `monthly care plan` -> `template/kit sold as a product` -> `course/ebook`.
