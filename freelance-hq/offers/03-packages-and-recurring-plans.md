# Packages, bundles and monthly plans

Single services are easy to compare on price. **Packages and monthly plans** are easier to sell, pay better and create steady income. Prices are hypotheses based on `BUSINESS-PLAN.md`; test them.

## 1. The three-door ladder (use for every client)
1. **Door 1: Free mini-audit (15 minutes).** `/audit`. Builds trust and shows 3 quick wins.
2. **Door 2: Paid audit or small starter** ($50-300 or a $20-100 setup). Low risk for the buyer.
3. **Door 3: Package + monthly plan.** The real income.

## 5. How to price (simple rules)
1. Start from `python3 scripts/price_calc.py quote` so you know the minimum.
2. Show three options; the middle one is the one you want.
3. Local clients: smaller one-off prices, more value in monthly plans and referrals. International: higher prices, fixed scope, platform escrow.
4. Take 50% up front on direct jobs; the balance before final files.
5. Raise prices by 15-30% after 3-5 happy clients.
6. Never discount without removing scope.
