# Bah.Digital master service menu (the full "everything I can offer" list)

This extends `../../bah-digital/docs/BUSINESS-PLAN.md` (your 5 launch offers, 10 divisions and prices) with every other service you can sell using Claude plus free tools, ranked by **how easy** and **how payable** they are.

## How to read this menu
- **Easy (E), 1-5:** can a beginner deliver good quality in about a week using Claude and free tools? 5 = very easy, 1 = hard/advanced.
- **Pay (P), 1-5:** how willing are buyers to pay a real price? 5 = high budgets and clear value.
- **Price:** "plan" = your Bah.Digital business plan numbers. "hyp" = my starting hypothesis to TEST. All in USD. Local = Sierra Leone SMEs; Intl = international clients.
- **Priority = E + P.** Sell the highest-scoring ones first (Tier A), add Tier B after your first reviews, and treat Tier C as later growth.

## One important idea: wide brand, narrow offers
Bah.Digital as a company can proudly list many services. But each *gig, profile, post and pitch* should lead with ONE clear offer, because buyers trust specialists. So:
- Your **website/offer sheet** shows the full menu (this file).
- Each **Fiverr gig / Upwork profile / outreach message** promotes ONE or TWO offers from Tier A.
- Use `/menu` to have Claude pick your launch pair.

## Do NOT offer (risk too high)
- Anything that helps cheat exams or submit someone else's work as theirs (ghost-written theses, fake assignments)
- Fake reviews, fake documents, forged certificates, impersonation
- Deepfakes or voice clones of real people without their clear consent
- Hacking, "recovering" accounts, or testing others' systems without written permission
- Medical, legal, tax or investment advice presented as professional advice
- Loans, crypto signals, get-rich schemes, or "guaranteed" results
- Spam, scraped personal data lists, or unsolicited bulk messaging
- Unofficial WhatsApp automation that violates WhatsApp's terms

## Best "first pair" combos for you
1. **Audit + Chatbot/Automation** (A1 -> A11/A10): the audit sells the build. Best for businesses with repeated questions or admin.
2. **Landing page + WhatsApp/Google setup** (A4 + A2 + A3): the easiest local package, "Get found and contacted in one week."
3. **Social graphics + short videos** (A5 + A9): quick, repeatable, retainer-friendly.
4. **Documents + decks + CV** (A8, A12, A14): large student/NGO/startup audience, fast cash while you build proof.

## Rules for every service
Written scope, 50% deposit for direct work, revision limit, honest AI disclosure where required, licensed assets only, no private client data in public repos, a monthly plan offered after delivery. Check every number in this table against real competitors before you publish prices.
