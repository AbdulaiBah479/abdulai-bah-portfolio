# Where to sell each service: local, regional and global

Your plan says "sell into both markets". Here is how, step by step. Everything is a hypothesis to test; verify platform access and payment options for your country.

## Market 1: Local (Sierra Leone and your own network)
**Who buys:** restaurants, hotels and guesthouses, shops, salons, schools, churches, clinics, real estate agents, NGOs, startups, students, event organisers, creators.
**What they usually need first:** to be found (Google Maps), to be contacted (WhatsApp), to look professional (logo, flyers, posts), to save time (simple automation, forms), to report to funders (NGOs).
**How to reach them (free):**
1. Walk in or message with a specific observation (use `/audit`).
2. WhatsApp and Facebook groups for businesses and communities (follow rules).
3. Campus, alumni and friends' networks (your university).
4. Referrals: after every job ask "who else needs this?" and offer a thank-you discount for the referrer.
5. Free case studies with friendly businesses (e.g. Green Vital, your client #1) in exchange for an honest testimonial.
6. Daily "building in public" posts: 3 tips, 1 before/after, 1 short video a week.
**How to price:** smaller one-off projects, more value in monthly plans. Accept Orange Money/Afrimoney/bank transfer with a 50% deposit. Send clear receipts.
**Messages to copy:** `templates/whatsapp-sales-messages.md`.
**Best local services:** A2, A3, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8, A9, A12, A14, B3, B4, B12, B13.

## Market 2: Regional / institutional (NGOs, schools, associations, startups)
**Needs:** reports for funders, simple data collection, dashboards, training, websites, documents, event materials.
**Steps:** find organisations through public directories and events; offer a small paid pilot (a dashboard, a form, a report template); document it as a case study; propose a monthly reporting/support plan.
**Services:** C5, B9, B13, B14, B19, B20, A8, A14.
**Caution:** data protection; get written permission before touching beneficiary data; use secure accounts; do not store sensitive data in public repos.

## Market 3: Global (platforms)
**Fiverr / Upwork:** productized gigs (Fiverr) and proposals (Upwork) for A4, A10, A11, A14, A5, A7, A8, A9, B2, B8, B16. Check which payout methods your country can use *before* you invest time.
**LinkedIn:** short posts and direct messages to small businesses abroad that need cheap, reliable help. Case studies are your proof.
**Contra / PeoplePerHour / others:** add later; one platform at a time.
**Agencies:** offer white-label help (design, landing pages, automation) under their brand.
**Products:** Gumroad/Payhip/Etsy/Lemon Squeezy for templates, kits, ebooks. Check payout support from Sierra Leone for each.

## Market 4: Your own audience
Newsletter + LinkedIn/TikTok/YouTube + free lead magnet ("20 AI automations for Sierra Leone businesses"). An audience makes every other market cheaper to win.

## Time split suggestion (first 90 days)
- 40% delivering paid work and pilots
- 30% finding clients (local outreach + platforms)
- 20% building proof and products (case studies, templates, ebook)
- 10% admin and learning

## Test plan (30 days)
1. Choose two Tier A services (`/menu`).
2. Local: 10 messages a day to businesses with a specific observation.
3. Global: 2 gigs live on Fiverr + 5 targeted Upwork proposals a day.
4. Record: contacts, replies, paid jobs, income in your private trackers.
5. After 30 days keep, adjust or replace. Move the winner into a package and a monthly plan.
