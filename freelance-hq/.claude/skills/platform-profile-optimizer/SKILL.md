# Platform profile optimizer

## Principles
- Headline = who you help + outcome + proof, not a job title. ("I build n8n automations that save agencies 10+ hrs/week")
- First 2 lines of the bio carry the whole pitch; most visitors read nothing else.
- Specific beats broad. One niche per profile; a generalist profile converts worse.
- Proof stack: numbers, screenshots, short case studies (problem, action, result), tools used, testimonials only if real.
- Never claim certifications, clients, or results that are not real.

## Steps
1. Ask which platform; read `cheatsheets/fiverr.md` or `cheatsheets/upwork.md` for current field limits (verify limits on the platform).
2. Pull real material from `../index.html` and `../bah-digital/` (portfolio projects: Farmer Marketplace, Smart Automation Pipeline, AI Chatbot System, Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra Campaign) and turn each into a case study: Problem, What I built, Stack, Result/what it proves.
3. Draft: headline (3 variants), bio (short + long), skills list ordered by search demand, services/specialised profiles, portfolio item descriptions, profile-photo and banner guidance.
4. Audit checklist: photo (clear face, plain background), keyword in title, response-time promise you can keep, availability, languages, rate strategy (see `playbooks/09-pricing-and-money.md`).
5. Save to `clients/_self/profiles/<platform>.md`; mark anything needing owner confirmation with `TODO(owner)`.
