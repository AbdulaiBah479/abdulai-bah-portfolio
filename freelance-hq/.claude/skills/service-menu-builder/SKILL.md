# Service menu builder

Read `../../bah-digital/docs/BUSINESS-PLAN.md`, `offers/02-bah-digital-service-menu.md`, `offers/03-packages-and-recurring-plans.md`, `offers/04-markets-local-and-global.md`, `my-plan.md` (if it exists) and the portfolio (`../index.html`).

## Steps
1. **Profile check (ask only what's missing):** hours per week, languages, devices, what the owner can deliver *well* today, who they already know (friends, Green Vital, university), payout options in their country.
2. **Filter** the menu to services the owner can honestly deliver in about a week with Claude and free tools. Remove anything on the "Do NOT offer" list.
3. **Score** the remaining services: Priority = Easy + Pay, then adjust for: proof available, local demand, global demand, recurring potential, and productization potential. Show a ranked top-10 table with reasons.
4. **Pick the launch pair** (one fast-cash + one asset-builder) and one monthly plan to attach. Explain why in plain language.
5. **Build the assets:** the one-page offer sheet (`templates/offer-sheet-one-page.md`), 2 packages with 3 tiers each (prices from the plan, adjusted for local vs international), a care plan (`templates/monthly-care-plan.md`), a Fiverr gig for each global-friendly service (`gig-builder`), a profile headline, and 10 WhatsApp/DM messages (`templates/whatsapp-sales-messages.md`).
6. **30-day test plan with numbers:** contacts per day, proposals per day, target replies/paid jobs/income; when to review.
7. **Compliance pass:** claims true, no fake proof, platform rules, AI disclosure, data protection, licences.
8. Save to `products/service-menu-<date>.md` (git-ignored) and tell the owner exactly what to do by hand today.

## Rules
Prices are hypotheses: say so and recommend checking real competitors. Never promise income. Start narrow even though the brand is broad: one offer per pitch. Prefer services where the owner already has portfolio proof (chatbot, automation, app, design).
