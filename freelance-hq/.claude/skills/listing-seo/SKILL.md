# Listing SEO

Marketplace search = intent match + conversion signals. Match the buyer's words, then earn clicks and sales.

## Steps
1. **Seed list**: 10 phrases a buyer would type (problem words, not your jargon).
2. **Expand with free sources**: marketplace search-box autocomplete, "related searches", Google autocomplete / People Also Ask, Amazon search suggestions (for KDP keyword slots), competitor titles and tags, Reddit/forum wording. Optional free tools: Google Keyword Planner, Google Trends, AnswerThePublic free tier, Pinterest Trends, Etsy search suggestions (verify tool availability).
3. **Score each phrase**: relevance (must be 5/5), demand signal (autocomplete presence, competitor volume), competition (reviews/sales of top results), fit with your price.
4. **Assign slots**: primary keyword in title front; supporting keywords across tags, subtitle, first lines of description, category; long-tail phrases for specificity. Use all tag slots with distinct phrases, no repeats.
5. **Write natural copy**: no stuffing, no misleading terms, no competitor/brand trademarks.
6. **Test and iterate**: change one thing per 2 weeks (title, first image, price), watch impressions -> clicks -> orders in `tracker/`.

## KDP specifics (verify)
7 keyword boxes, up to 3 categories (via category requests), subtitle is searchable, front-load the main phrase in title/subtitle.

## Output
A table: keyword | intent | demand | competition | where used. Saved beside the listing file.
