# Course builder

## Steps
1. **Promise:** "After this course, a [learner] can [specific result] in [time]." Validate demand (`niche-validator`) and ask 5 prospective learners what stops them.
2. **Prove you can teach it:** you must have *done* the thing at least once. Use your real project as the course spine ("build along").
3. **Curriculum:** 5-8 short lessons (5-12 minutes), each = one outcome, one demo, one exercise. Add a quick-win lesson first and a "common mistakes" lesson.
4. **Scripts:** for each lesson: hook, steps, demo notes, recap, exercise. Keep language simple; add captions/subtitles (Whisper can help).
5. **Materials:** workbook/PDF (Pandoc/Typst/Canva), templates, checklists, starter files.
6. **Production (free tools):** OBS for screen recording, DaVinci Resolve/CapCut for editing, good audio first, slides via Marp or Canva.
7. **Platform:** start with Gumroad/Payhip (own the customer list, higher margin); Udemy/Skillshare for discovery (they control pricing/promo rules; verify terms).
8. **Pricing:** mini-course $19-79; with feedback or live Q&A, $99-299 (test). Early-bird price for first 20 buyers; offer a refund policy.
9. **Launch:** pre-sell to your list, free lesson as a lead magnet, community feedback, testimonials from beta students.
10. **Improve:** update from student questions; add a bonus module; bundle with your ebook.

## Quality gate
Learner can reproduce the result from scratch; files tested; audio clear; claims honest; licences checked; support channel defined.
