# Local business audit

Audit only PUBLIC information. Be kind: the owner is a person, not a target.

## Steps
1. Inputs: business name/URL/location. Fetch their public site and listing pages (WebFetch/WebSearch). Never log in, scrape private data, or guess personal contacts.
2. Check: mobile load and layout, clear contact/booking path, offer clarity in 5 seconds, trust signals (reviews, photos, address), Google Business info and review replies, social freshness, missing basics (HTTPS, title/meta, accessibility), repetitive customer questions that a chatbot/automation could handle.
3. Score each area 1-5 with one-line evidence (screenshot-able). If it is already excellent, say so and skip outreach.
4. Pick **3 quick wins** that can be done in under an hour each, ranked by impact, plus 1 bigger opportunity linked to your offers (`offers/01-offers-catalog.md`).
5. Write the one-page audit (`clients/_prospects/<slug>/audit.md`): summary, scorecard, 3 quick wins with "how", opportunity with estimated benefit (label estimates).
6. Draft a 4-line email using `templates/cold-email-sequence.md` (observation, impact, offer of a free walkthrough, opt-out). Never claim you have a relationship or private data.
7. Log in `tracker/leads.csv` (stage: Identified).

## Rules
Truthful, no negative shaming, comply with anti-spam laws (identity, opt-out), do not email personal addresses harvested from non-public sources, honour requests to stop.
