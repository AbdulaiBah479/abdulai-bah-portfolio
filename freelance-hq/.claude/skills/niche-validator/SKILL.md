# Niche validator

Goal: choose ONE offer to test for 30 days, backed by evidence, not hunches.

## Inputs to collect (ask only what is missing)
Skills and proof (portfolio items), hours per week, minimum income target, languages, tools already owned, platforms already joined.

## Steps
1. **Skill x Problem x Buyer grid.** List 5 skills and for each the painful, paid problem it solves and who pays (e.g. "n8n automation -> small agencies drowning in manual lead follow-up").
2. **Demand evidence** (use WebSearch/WebFetch, never invent numbers):
   - Fiverr: search the service, note number of results, price range of top 10 gigs, review counts of mid-ranked gigs (reviews on a 6-month-old gig = proven demand).
   - Upwork: count recent job posts, budgets, repeat keywords.
   - Digital products: Etsy/Gumroad/Creative Market bestseller signals, Google autocomplete, Reddit/forum pain-point threads.
   - Ebooks: Amazon category bestseller rank, review counts, "Look inside" gaps.
3. **Competition gap.** What do the top 10 all do the same? What do buyers complain about in reviews? Pick a wedge (speed, niche industry, guarantee, bundle, language, price tier).
4. **Margin and effort score** (1-5 each): demand, competition gap, proof available, delivery hours per sale, repeatability/productizability, personal interest.
5. **Verdict table** with weighted total; recommend the top 1, a backup, and what to kill.
6. **Offer sketch**: one-sentence promise, 3 tiers (Basic/Standard/Premium), price anchors, delivery time, proof needed.
7. **Test plan**: 30-day experiment with a numeric success threshold (e.g. 15 proposals sent, 3 replies, 1 sale) and the date to decide.

## Output
Save to `products/niche-<slug>.md`. Add a row to `tracker/gigs.csv` or `tracker/products.csv`. See `playbooks/00-strategy-and-niche.md`.
