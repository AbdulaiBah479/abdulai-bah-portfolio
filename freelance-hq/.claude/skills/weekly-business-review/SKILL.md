# Weekly business review

## Steps
1. Run `python3 scripts/income_report.py` (income by platform/month, fees, net). Read `tracker/leads.csv`, `gigs.csv`, `products.csv`, `content_calendar.csv`.
2. **Funnel numbers** (week and trailing 4 weeks): proposals sent, replies, calls, won; gig impressions -> clicks -> orders; product views -> sales; email subscribers added.
3. **Diagnose**: low replies = proposal/targeting problem; low clicks = title/thumbnail; low conversion = price/offer/proof; low repeat = delivery/follow-up.
4. **Decide** (one change per bottleneck): Double down (what worked), Fix (one test), Stop (what drained time).
5. **Hours audit**: billable vs marketing vs admin; effective hourly rate = net income / total hours. Compare with target in `cheatsheets/pricing-formulas.md`.
6. **Next 7 days**: max 5 priorities, each with a number (e.g. "send 10 proposals", "publish 2 gigs", "write 1 ebook chapter") and day.
7. **Admin**: invoices due, taxes set-aside (see `playbooks/10-legal-tax-payments.md`), backup, expiring subscriptions, platform ratings/response rate.
8. Write the review to `tracker/reviews/YYYY-WW.md` (create folder) in under one page.
