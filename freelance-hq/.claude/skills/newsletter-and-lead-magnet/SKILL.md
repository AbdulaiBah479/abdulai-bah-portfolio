# Newsletter and lead magnet

## Steps
1. **Audience + promise:** one reader and one weekly benefit ("one automation idea every Tuesday").
2. **Lead magnet:** solves one small problem fast. Options: checklist, template, 10-page mini-ebook, spreadsheet calculator, sample workflow. Name it by result ("The 15-minute Automation Audit").
3. **Tool:** free-tier email service (Kit, MailerLite, Beehiiv; verify limits) or self-host listmonk later. Create signup form + double opt-in where required.
4. **Welcome sequence:** `templates/launch-email-sequence.md` (5 emails over 8 days).
5. **Weekly issue format:** hook line, one idea, one story/case, one action, one CTA. 300-500 words.
6. **Growth:** link in every profile, pinned post, end of every ebook, on thank-you pages, partner swaps, guest posts.
7. **Compliance:** only email people who opted in; include sender identity, unsubscribe link, privacy notice; keep consent records; no purchased lists.
8. **Metrics:** signups/week, open rate, click rate, replies, unsubscribes; run subject-line tests.
9. **Monetise later:** own products, service offers, affiliate links (disclosed), sponsor slots.

Save assets in `products/lead-magnet-<slug>/` and log in `tracker/content_calendar.csv`.
