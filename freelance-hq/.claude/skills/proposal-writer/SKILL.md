# Proposal writer

Read `playbooks/02-upwork.md` and `cheatsheets/proposal-formulas.md`.

## Steps
1. **Triage the job (30 seconds)**. Score: budget fit, clarity, client history (spend, hire rate, reviews), red flags (`cheatsheets/client-red-flags.md`), skills match. If it scores low, say "skip" and why. Sending fewer, better proposals wins.
2. **Extract**: the real problem behind the post, the exact words the client used, success criteria, deadline, tools named.
3. **Write (150-250 words)** with the HOOK formula:
   - **H**ook: first 2 lines mirror their problem and show you read it (shown in the preview, so they must earn the click).
   - **O**ffer: your approach in 3 concrete steps, not your life story.
   - **O**ne proof: the most relevant real project with a result.
   - **K**ey question: one sharp, specific question that shows expertise and invites reply.
   - CTA: propose a 10-minute call or a paid mini-first-step.
4. **Pricing line**: fixed price or hourly with the reasoning, optional smaller starter milestone. Use `cheatsheets/pricing-formulas.md`.
5. **Screening questions**: answer every one, specifically.
6. Output 2 variants (direct, consultative) + recommended bid + Connects/cost note (verify current Connects cost on Upwork).
7. Log in `tracker/leads.csv` (status: sent). Set a follow-up date, 3-4 days later.

## Never
Copy-paste generic templates, promise unrealistic speed, bash competitors, or include contact details that the platform forbids.
